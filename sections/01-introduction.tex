\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Encryption conceals content while serving caches, packet streams and tool executions expose other observations \cite{earlybird2025,netecho2025,jeong2026wra}. A deployment can keep the name of a defense while changing what an observer sees, which state its cover affects, or how the attacker is trained. Choosing a mechanism is therefore only the start of a deployment decision. The next question is: \emph{which protection, function and cost evidence can be retained across the proposed change?}

\paragraph{One defense name can refer to different evidence}
CachePrune's selective-sharing results can inform a configuration-specific feasibility assessment \cite[5.1--5.6]{wu2026cacheprune}. Early Bird's prefix policy and semantic-cache prototype require separate cost decisions \cite[V-A--B]{earlybird2025}. These distinctions follow from the observer, primitive, operating point and measurement object. A shared mechanism or dataset name does not establish paired observations. The useful outcome is a scoped candidate and a measurement decision.

\paragraph{The systematization follows three boundaries}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Receiving view.} A protected representation can be followed by another emitter or observable. Retain the exact receiver and permitted fields, not a blanket privacy label.
\item \textbf{Affected state.} Discarding a reply and preventing an action's effects are different operations. Retain a content-preservation witness where it applies; separately establish task outcomes for added execution.
\item \textbf{Attacker and workload.} A clean-trained classifier, a defense-aware attacker and a source-calibrated defense establish different objects. Retain their fitting conditions and holdout units with their measured function and cost.
\end{itemize}
These boundaries organize the paper's contribution: a source-bound account of \emph{defense-evidence reuse}. The evidence card records the protected target, receiver, intervention, attacker, function and cost together with their source locations. Record-level joins establish which measurements describe the same configuration; the transfer audit establishes what was held fixed or refitted. Their output is a proposed, inspectable assessment of what to retain, separate or remeasure (Table~\ref{tab:rq-findings}). No analyst comparison, independent coding study or deployment trial establishes that this procedure improves decisions, agreement or efficiency.

\paragraph{Source selection limits the empirical claims}
The original auxiliary-channel search links 19 privacy/attribution mitigation families to evaluated records, including both attack-oriented and defense-oriented studies. A purposive defense-first challenge set adds four named seeds and two selected sources representing cache isolation, protected interfaces, communication shaping and agent privacy. Focused hardware readings further expose evidence outside that core frame. These sources inform the conceptual classification and practical decisions; they do not repair selection bias or support field-wide reporting rates. We keep their receiving views and measurement scopes separate; counts and source-removal sensitivities remain reconstructable from the cards.

\paragraph{Worked evidence makes the decisions testable}
The synthesis builds on website-fingerprinting and adaptive-evaluation practice \cite{juarez2014critical,mathews2023critical,tramer2020adaptive,carlini2019evaluating}. Its LLM/agent-specific contribution connects prefix and semantic caches, authorized message content and representation choice, and discarded responses and executed effects. Two new studies make the latter decisions concrete. Fixed-weight generation followed by timed TLS loopback supplies an exploratory synthetic example of attacker adaptation, while its calibration audit distinguishes identical-parameter controls from conditional source comparisons. Fresh three-arm retail-sandbox interactions measure task utility when an extra read or same-item write is hidden from the planner. The cases instantiate reporting distinctions rather than test their usefulness to analysts. Existing external applications comprise 15 measurement records for 14 defense--study pairs across three published WF studies; their outcomes were visible when coded. The synthesis presents selected source-to-assessment examples, while the supplement retains the full ledger. Experiment results and collection provenance remain in Appendix~\ref{app:boundary-experiments} and the supplement.
