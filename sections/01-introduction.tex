\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Encryption conceals message content, yet LLM engines and agents expose packet sizes, cache timing, visited domains and tool sequences \cite{weiss2024prompt,promptpeek2025,earlybird2025,agentprint2025,uitraces2026,jeong2026wra}. Defenses follow: padding, batching, cache isolation, decoy queries and action humanization. Each is evaluated at one receiver, execution boundary and workload, yet its headline number is cited as a property of the mechanism. A padding result measured on an upstream link says nothing about a frontend that re-emits decoded text; decoys whose read responses are discarded say nothing about cover that executes writes; and the overhead of a semantic cache does not price prefix isolation reported in the same paper.

We therefore systematize defense evidence at the level of the \emph{evaluated result record} rather than the paper. A record binds one outcome to its target, receiver projection, intervention boundary, attacker fit, workload and cost object (\S\ref{sec:scope}). Three instruments operate on these records: a map of 82 study families by signal origin, receiving view and threat polarity; a nine-item audit of what each mitigation family binds; and a six-axis matrix of reported holdouts. Together they answer one question: \emph{which changes invalidate a measured defense result?}

\paragraph{Contributions}
This paper contributes a systematization of defense-evidence reuse. The family map locates the signal and receiver, the record audit establishes which outcomes can be compared or joined, and the holdout synthesis identifies the deployment changes covered by the evidence. Three connected results follow:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A result-level evidence model and field map (\S\S\ref{sec:methodology}--\ref{sec:corpus-map}).} We map 82 study families and retain their receiver, threat polarity and evaluation provenance. Separating a family's outcomes into evaluated records keeps execution mode, protected state and cost attached to the configuration that produced them.
\item \textbf{A complete comparison and binding audit (\S\ref{sec:defenses}).} We examine all 741 pairs of the 39 records. Among 133 related pairs, 17 permit a source-qualified descriptive comparison; eight of 15 same-family pairs concern different primitives, targets or costs. Record-local joins identify CachePrune's single inference--utility--cost package through two records, rather than assembling a package from unrelated family-level cells.
\item \textbf{A transfer-axis synthesis and reuse procedure (\S\S\ref{sec:evaluation}--\ref{sec:discussion}).} The 82-family, six-axis holdout map distinguishes input separation from evidence for changed models, implementations and environments. Table~\ref{tab:r19-decisions} translates the literature synthesis into minimum evidence requirements for each deployment change.
\end{itemize}
