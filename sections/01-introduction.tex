\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Encryption conceals message content, yet LLM engines and agents expose packet sizes, cache timing, visited domains and tool sequences \cite{weiss2024prompt,promptpeek2025,earlybird2025,agentprint2025,uitraces2026,jeong2026wra}. Defenses follow: padding, batching, cache isolation, decoy queries and action humanization. Each is evaluated at one receiver, execution boundary and workload, yet its headline number is cited as a property of the mechanism. A padding result measured on an upstream link says nothing about a frontend that re-emits decoded text; decoys whose read responses are discarded say nothing about cover that executes writes; and the overhead of a semantic cache does not price prefix isolation reported in the same paper.

We systematize defense evidence around three operations: \emph{compare result records, join outcomes at one configuration, and decide what evidence a deployment change requires}. A result record binds one outcome to its target, receiver projection, intervention boundary, attacker fit, workload and cost object (\S\ref{sec:scope}). The 82-family map locates these records in the field; the nine-item audit and six-axis holdout matrix establish their evidence scope. Together they answer one question: \emph{which defense claims does the literature support when a deployment changes?} Transfer boundaries here delimit the scope of an evaluated result and the evidence needed to extend it.

\paragraph{Contributions}
The SoK contributes three connected results, each grounded in the complete literature audit:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Record-level comparison (\S\S\ref{sec:scope}--\ref{sec:defenses}).} The complete inventory of 741 record pairs separates supported descriptive comparisons from object mismatches and unresolved evidence. Within-family contrasts expose substitutions of primitive, target and cost that paper-level summaries conceal. Cohort accounting and family-deletion checks make the dependence on grouping and record multiplicity inspectable.
\item \textbf{Configuration-level joint binding (\S\ref{sec:defenses}).} The 351 record--item cells identify which protection, utility and cost outcomes describe the same evaluated intervention. CachePrune's two records supply one inference--utility--cost package at a shared configuration. Deduplicating that package keeps record counts, family coverage and jointly supported operating points distinct.
\item \textbf{Deployment decisions (\S\S\ref{sec:evaluation}--\ref{sec:discussion}).} The 82-family, six-axis holdout map identifies which deployment changes the sources evaluate. Table~\ref{tab:r19-decisions} combines that map with comparison and joint-binding judgments to specify which evidence can be retained and which measurement a proposed extension requires.
\end{itemize}
