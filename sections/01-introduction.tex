\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Encryption conceals message content, yet LLM engines and agents expose packet sizes, cache timing, visited domains and tool sequences \cite{weiss2024prompt,promptpeek2025,earlybird2025,agentprint2025,uitraces2026,jeong2026wra}. A defense result applies to a particular receiver, execution boundary and workload. Reusing a headline score across a frontend, dispatcher or tool provider can change both its protected signal and its cost object.

Our unit is the \emph{evaluated result record}. Separating Dangol's stored/live results prevents subtracting unmatched scores; separating Early Bird's KV-prefix and semantic-cache mechanisms prevents borrowing overhead. Observer access, threat polarity and execution state explain why read-only privacy cover and state-changing evasion have different utility consequences.

\paragraph{Central question and contributions}
\emph{Which changes invalidate a measured defense result?} The map locates systems, records bind operating points, and the audit identifies transfer obligations.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Systematic transfer evidence.} The complete 39-record census separates six trace transformations, five simulation/estimate results, six component/calibration measurements, 20 executed collections/prototypes and two unresolved placements. A six-axis, 82-family matrix distinguishes reported holdouts from condition sweeps and unresolved evidence.
\item \textbf{Target-dependent calibration transfer.} Moving MiniMax calibration to GLM changes cancellation/return BA from 50.0/67.3\% to 70.8/50.0\%. Protection shifts in opposite directions; a single aggregate transfer score hides this operating-point change.
\item \textbf{Localization and utility boundaries.} Write-only shaping costs 1.45--1.51$\times$ bytes but leaves return BA at 61.3--61.6\%; detector precision differs sharply across endpoints. Fresh deadline-controlled planners succeed on 37/50 versus 33/50 tasks with prefix cover. These measurements determine what oracle localization and logical state equivalence actually license.
\end{itemize}
