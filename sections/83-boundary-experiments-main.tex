\subsection{E4: finite synthetic replay and conditional calibration comparisons}
Pinned qwen3:8b, qwen3:0.6b and llama3.2:1b each generate five templates for 100 synthetic topics under a 128-token budget. The binary target is 50 medical versus 50 nonmedical topics. Each of five test folds holds out ten topics of each class with all their templates; chance BA is 50\%. Saved native-chunk schedules are sent through timed TLS~1.3 loopback with byte-identical decoded content. Observations cover response application-record lengths and timing, excluding request and handshake traffic. The controls are buckets, chunk batches, variable-stop fixed cells and a hard application-event cap; overflow-retaining minimum-count padding is a separate ablation. None measures a defense executing during model generation, WAN behavior or real-user topic inference.

Calibration uses training folds. The original $D_0/D_2$ suite separately maximizes OOF BA over logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting, fitted on clean/defended data and evaluated on the same defended folds. The raw, $D_0$ and $D_2$ suite values select winners separately; choosing and reporting each winner on those same OOF predictions can be optimistic. The subsequent fixed-logistic comparison and separate topic holdout are reported in Section~\ref{sec:discussion} and the supplement; both reuse a dataset already visible during earlier analysis. Full12 additionally includes metadata/profiling attacks and the majority baseline. The original suite maxima remain exploratory; their topic bootstrap conditions on fitted attackers and omits model refitting and calibration-sampling uncertainty. Winner reselection among saved predictions does not supply an independent evaluation of model selection.

At the default source comparisons, all 45 source--target--fold combinations use a 32-byte bucket quantum, four chunks per batch, or 32-byte cells at 50~ms ticks, respectively. Batching is fixed by design; the source-based bucket and cell quantiles round to the same values. Their zero differences consequently provide no calibration-transfer evidence. The repeated matrices and bucket display remain in the supplement as identical-parameter controls.

Table~\ref{tab:r46-hardcap-conditional} reports all six hard-cap imported-minus-local contrasts. Some primary source/fold comparisons have distinct caps: Qwen~8B and Qwen~0.6B differ in three folds but both use 87 events in fold IDs 1 and 3; Llama~1B uses 128 in every fold. The paired topic intervals hold fitted attackers fixed. Secondary sign-flip tests additionally fix observed winners and assume sign symmetry or exchangeability; Holm covers only the two imports for each target, not all targets, controls or operating points. Neither refitting nor winner-selection uncertainty is included in those tests. A subsequent topic-resampling control quantifies local cap-parameter variation (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}), but does not rerun attacks under each refit; these comparisons remain exploratory. At the reported precision, five of the six intervals include or touch zero. The one interval clearly excluding zero is conditional and increases attack BA; it does not establish robust portability or a general source effect. Failure to exclude zero in the others does not establish equivalence.


The original queue retains 34,440 successful captures, one unknown outcome and 559 never-started jobs. Recovery collected the latter and a post hoc 500-session Qwen~8B fixed-cell repeat with the same local TLS implementation in a new runtime/batch. Among 495 matched sessions, bytes and record counts agree; the median topic-mean completion difference is $-0.461$~ms. This does not establish exchangeability or remove batch effects. The original unknown remains unknown. The proxy/direct-path and concurrency changes below concern R44, not E4.

\subsection{R44: fresh online task utility under added execution}
The mutable Qwen service alias supplies 561 fresh episodes in 187 three-arm groups across 98 nonrandomly selected tasks; 113 complete GLM groups remain separate. The selected subset uses randomized execution and arm permutations, not random cohort selection. Each arm has fresh planner/user interactions and a separate state clone; extra calls are selected without gold-action information, and unexposed episodes remain included. All 97 executed cover writes consume modification quota and change state, making this a state-changing stress condition. No online side-effect-free write arm separates extra-call effects from payment-ledger and quota semantics; reference-program shadow tests are separate. Fixed-weight, random-cohort and multiple-domain replications are not established.

Episode and task-weighted estimates are distinct. Task-bootstrap intervals condition on the selected cohort; sign-flip tests assume task-level sign symmetry (Holm over two contrasts). The read interval is not an equivalence bound.

Recovery changed concurrency from 10 to 2 and the transport path; ten interrupted requests have unknown waiting times and server outcomes. Recorded time excludes those waits and is not complete total cost.
