\subsection{E4: finite synthetic replay and conditional calibration comparisons}
Pinned qwen3:8b, qwen3:0.6b and llama3.2:1b each generate five templates for 100 synthetic topics under a 128-token budget. The binary target is 50 medical versus 50 nonmedical topics. Each of five test folds holds out ten topics of each class with all their templates; chance BA is 50\%. Saved native-chunk schedules are sent through timed TLS~1.3 loopback with byte-identical decoded content. Observations cover response application-record lengths and timing, excluding request and handshake traffic. The controls are buckets, chunk batches, variable-stop fixed cells and a hard application-event cap; overflow-retaining minimum-count padding is a separate ablation. None measures a defense executing during model generation, WAN behavior or real-user topic inference.

Calibration uses training folds. $D_0/D_2$ separately maximize OOF BA over logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting, fitted on clean/defended data and evaluated on the same defended folds. The raw, $D_0$ and $D_2$ values use the same prespecified learner classes but select their winners separately; they do not report a paired effect or interval for one fixed classifier. Choosing and reporting each winner on those same OOF predictions can be optimistic. Full12 additionally includes metadata/profiling attacks and the majority baseline. These are exploratory suite maxima; the topic bootstrap conditions on fitted attackers and omits model refitting and calibration-sampling uncertainty. Winner reselection among saved predictions does not supply an independent evaluation of model selection.

At the default source comparisons, all 45 source--target--fold combinations use a 32-byte bucket quantum, four chunks per batch, or 32-byte cells at 50~ms ticks, respectively. Batching is fixed by design; the source-based bucket and cell quantiles round to the same values. Their zero differences consequently provide no calibration-transfer evidence. The repeated matrices and bucket display remain in the supplement as identical-parameter controls.

Table~\ref{tab:r46-hardcap-conditional} reports all six hard-cap imported-minus-local contrasts. Some primary source/fold comparisons have distinct caps: Qwen~8B and Qwen~0.6B differ in three folds but both use 87 events in fold IDs 1 and 3; Llama~1B uses 128 in every fold. The paired topic intervals hold fitted attackers fixed. Secondary sign-flip tests additionally fix observed winners and assume sign symmetry or exchangeability; Holm covers only the two imports for each target, not all targets, controls or operating points. Neither refitting nor winner-selection uncertainty is included in those tests. A local fitting-variability control is not reported, so these comparisons remain exploratory. At the reported precision, five of the six intervals include or touch zero. The one interval clearly excluding zero is conditional and increases attack BA; it does not establish robust portability or a general source effect. Failure to exclude zero in the others does not establish equivalence.


The original base queue retains 34,440 successful captures, one unknown terminal outcome and 559 never-started jobs. Recovery separately collected the latter and a post hoc 500-session whole-arm repeat for the affected Qwen~8B fixed-cell point. The recovery collector used the same local TLS implementation in a new runtime/batch. Among 495 old/new matched sessions, bytes and record counts agree; the median across topic-mean completion differences is $-0.461$~ms. This descriptive agreement does not establish exchangeability or eliminate batch effects. The original unknown outcome stays unknown. The proxy/direct-path and concurrency changes below belong to R44, not E4.

\subsection{R44: fresh online task utility under added execution}
The mutable Qwen service alias supplies 561 fresh episodes in 187 three-arm groups from 98 nonrandomly selected tasks; the 113 complete GLM groups remain separate. The protocol records randomized execution order and arm permutations within the selected subset, not random selection of that cohort. Each arm has fresh planner/user interactions and a separate state clone. Extra calls are selected without gold-action information, and unexposed episodes remain included. All 97 executed cover writes have state effects and consume modification quota. This chosen API therefore supplies a strong state-changing stress condition. R44 has no online side-effect-free write arm to separate generic extra-call effects from its payment-ledger and quota semantics; prior reference-program shadow tests are a different experiment. No fixed-weight, randomized-cohort or multiple-domain replication is established.

The table separates episode success counts from task-weighted estimates. Task-bootstrap intervals condition on the selected cohort; sign-flip tests assume task-level sign symmetry, with Holm adjustment over two contrasts. The read interval is not an equivalence bound.

Recovery changed effective concurrency from 10 to 2 and the transport path. Ten interrupted requests have unknown waiting times and server-side outcomes. Recorded time excludes those unknown waits and does not constitute complete total cost.
