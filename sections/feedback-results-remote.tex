\subsection{Separate hosted-model retest}
The hosted cohort uses 96 tasks per condition, with 36/12/48 training/validation/test decisions and sixteen nonempty test sessions. Its mutable alias and tunnel observation change the backend and network together. All 192 attempts have terminal outcomes; five invalid model outputs remain in the recognition denominators.

Against the 8/48 majority reference, LR scores 5/48 before feedback, 15/48 after Full feedback and 10/48 after Projected feedback. k-FP scores 3/48, 6/48 and 7/48; its Full--Projected direction reverses. None of the four primary Holm-adjusted comparisons crosses .05. The LR difference is 10.4 points with an unadjusted training/test-refit interval of $[-3.2,41.5]$. Complete-execution model-only LR is 9/48 in both conditions (Table~\ref{tab:phase-complete}). The retest supplies no consistent intervention effect across the declared attackers and endpoints.

Held-out completion is 46/48 for Full and 47/48 for Projected. The model-byte ratio is .932 with paired interval $[.868,1.003]$. Supplement S1 reports this cohort separately from the local study.
