\section{Synthesis: From Evidence to Reuse Decisions}\label{sec:discussion}
The census (\S\ref{sec:defenses}) covers all 39 records and the holdout matrix (\S\ref{sec:evaluation}) all 82 families. We now select three examples by the \emph{comparison decision} they expose, not by effect size: a changed receiving path, cover with different state effects, and an adaptation/cost mismatch within a named mechanism. They explain mechanisms within the complete census and motivate the selected controlled measurements in \S\ref{sec:controlled-placement}.

\subsection{Case 1: bind the final target-bearing emitter}
NetEcho's upstream packet-size shaping operates on a different projection from the tool-call sequence. Its plaintext-aware frontend then emits a new size trace (grouped token counts or character lengths), reopening a size channel after upstream protection \cite{netecho2025}. SplitDecoy is a separate return-path record: the cloud sees nonzero gradient row support on every tested frame across nine seeds \cite[Sections 5.1--5.3]{politis2026splitdecoy}. Both change the receiver boundary and therefore require a separately bound inference result.

The data processing inequality requires $S\to Z\to Y$: target-oblivious postprocessing cannot increase $I(S;Z)$ \cite[Theorem~2.8.1]{cover2006elements}. Plaintext-aware re-emission or backward computation breaks that premise. Exp~1 measures what restoring it costs under buffered and streaming emission.

\subsection{Case 2: resolve the WRA versus AHB cover asymmetry}
WRA's one-decoy $E_{\rm func}$ drops from 0.770 to 0.610, whereas AHB's Trip Planning success falls from 0.75 to 0.15 under \texttt{Online+Fake} humanization \cite{jeong2026wra,zhu2026turing}. Grouping both as ``cover traffic'' conceals different objectives and executions.

WRA discards decoy reads for Type~I confidentiality; AHB executes GUI events for Type~III evasion, potentially navigating or submitting forms. Their utility outcomes are not contradictory, and AHB's missing condition-specific denominators preclude a matched causal effect. A shared workflow setting does not establish shared state effects. Terminal-state reward and dispatch-time authorization also answer different questions: an unconfirmed write can still reach the intended final state. Exp~2 checks both.

\subsection{Case 3: bind adaptive retraining and cost objects}
A matched adaptation contrast compares clean-trained $D_0$ and defense-trained $D_2$ on identical defended tests. UI Traces and Dangol raise the record-binding question this leaves open: whether a refit, a measured delay and a utility outcome refer to the same executed condition \cite{uitraces2026,dangol2026workflow}. Cost objects show the same pattern. Early Bird's $6$ms/$4\%$ GPTCache cost does not price $K$-prefix isolation; KVGov's $1.3\,\mu$s HMAC microcost omits lost reuse; SpliceDefense's QCP cost does not price CTBF+QCP \cite{earlybird2025,addagada2026kvgov,spliceleak2026}. CachePrune binds protection, QA/summarization utility and throughput, while OTRO separates conditional leakage from TTFT/padding costs \cite{wu2026cacheprune,lee2026otro}. Exps~3--4 test calibration, refitting and timing against one cohort and matched observation sets.
