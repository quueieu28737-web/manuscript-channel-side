\section{Which Evidence Supports Defense Transfer?}\label{sec:evaluation}\label{sec:threats}
\subsection{Separate the evaluated object before counting holdouts}
The transfer audit distinguishes three questions: does an \emph{attack} recognize new inputs or systems; is a \emph{defense condition} evaluated; and does a \emph{fixed attacker or defense calibration} carry from a specified source to a target? Table~\ref{tab:r26-transfer-objects} makes the distinction operational. Repeating a defense on several models can establish condition-specific outcomes without establishing a held-out model or transferring a fitted calibration.

\subsection{A changed generator and a tested defense are different bindings}
LLM-Fin, ArchSnoop and LeakyLMs evaluate recognition of unseen model variants or architectures \cite{nazari2024llmfin,weng2026archsnoop,majidi2026leakylms}. This supplies attack-side model generalization. In the traffic benchmark, graph-to-star and star-to-graph classification explicitly move the fitted attacker across topologies; the prompt/temperature countermeasures are separate evaluations \cite[V-B--C]{pouryousef2026traffic}. Dangol's leave-composition-out test likewise examines recognition of a novel workflow shape, separately from its protected-capture frontier \cite[9.8--9.12]{dangol2026workflow}. The reusable result in both topology entries is the recognizer's tested generalization.

There is nevertheless useful \emph{defense-side variation} evidence. Time Will Tell v1 tests a 60-word-explanation instruction on three selected high-bias classification tasks with unchanged three-shot examples and perfect output-token-count observation. Reported mean attack success rate (ASR) changes from 88.2\% to 61.9\% for GPT-4o and from 79.3\% to 80.3\% for Gemma2-9b-IT \cite[VII-B]{timewilltell2024}. This supports condition-specific variation under a common instruction, not an adapted remote-defense test. Separate padding estimates do not establish matched protected task quality and cost. Whisper Leak evaluates mitigation conditions across provider-models, while its prompt holdout describes the classifier protocol \cite[3.2,6]{whisperleak2025}. Preserve those per-condition results; a shared rule, a refitted local attack, a source-trained attack and a source-calibrated defense are four distinct comparisons.

\paragraph{Deployment decision}
For the proposed change, name the source, target, held-out unit, quantities held fixed or refitted, and protected outcome. Carry forward only the corresponding binding. An attack-generalization result justifies including that attacker at the target; a defended-condition result supplies its local outcome; a source-to-target defense-calibration result supports only the stated transfer. This prevents both overclaiming portability and discarding useful local evidence. Unresolved hardware/time entries in the discovery matrix identify source follow-up, not absent experiments.
