\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK turns dispersed defense evidence into traceable comparisons, configuration-bound outcomes and deployment measurement requirements. Its three contributions---record-level comparison, configuration-level joint binding and deployment decisions---connect a reuse claim to its supporting observations. The 133 within-question judgments expose changes of primitive, target and cost within shared family names. Configuration-linked joins distinguish a usable protection--utility--cost package from outcomes reported at different operating points. The six-axis synthesis separates attack generalization from defended-condition testing and transported calibration. The cache-reuse example shows the positive path: retain a supported candidate and its quality/cost package, then identify the specific measurement needed for another candidate.

External retrospective cases and seven internal judgments make these decisions concrete. Record witnesses, query membership and executable checks expose their evidential basis. The practical contribution is a traceable path from published outcomes to the measurements required by a proposed deployment.
