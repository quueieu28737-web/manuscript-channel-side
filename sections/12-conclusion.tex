\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK synthesizes mitigation results within an empirical attack-evaluation source frame. Outcome-specific applicability separates source-supported preservation from interventions that can alter content or execution. Functional quality is explicitly bound in 3/8 applicable families, or 4/8 with partial evidence; eleven are exempt from that narrow query. The four agent-associated families leave same-condition end-to-end task scores unbound, with only WRA clearly adding executed decoy research calls.

CachePrune and SparLeak provide measured quality examples; lossless framing supplies a different, narrower functional witness. The decision procedure retains the receiving view, execution state and evaluation condition with each result. Controlled attacker comparisons show that a protection estimate can also change after fitting on defended data. These distinctions specify which result to retain and which deployment outcome to measure.
