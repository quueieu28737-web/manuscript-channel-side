\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Transfer boundaries organize defense evidence into deployment decisions. The 39-record audit exposes eight within-family object substitutions and separates joint operating-point evidence from individual family bindings. Experiments price the consequences of changing emitters, executed state, calibration and attacker observations. Calibration transfer yields a resolved return reduction alongside an uncertain cancellation change and higher byte cost. The decision table specifies the minimum re-evaluation for each boundary, making source-bound systematization actionable.
