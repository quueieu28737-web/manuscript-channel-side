\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This methodological position paper applies established evaluation principles to source-version, observation, execution and configuration links in LLM/agent side-channel research. Its reporting cards and bounded reappraisals make particular evidence joins inspectable; their benefit to analysts and superiority over existing guidance remain untested. The finite synthetic replay illustrates attacker-fitting sensitivity but does not establish a robust calibration-transfer effect. The nonrandom retail-sandbox cohort quantifies a deliberately state-changing write condition, not a general effect of cover traffic. These scoped outputs retain source assumptions and unresolved measurements without claiming complete literature coverage or validated deployment decisions.
