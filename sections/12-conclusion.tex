\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
In the inspected privacy/attribution mitigation sources, inference results are more frequently bound than function or matched fitting results. Accepting partial function evidence changes the count from 3/16 to 5/16. The SoK connects these reporting patterns to source-specific deployment decisions.

CachePrune and SparLeak provide positive configuration-bound examples. The decision procedure retains the receiving view, execution state and evaluation condition with each result. Controlled examples show why the attacker belongs to that condition: fitting on defended data restores signal for some shaping schemes, whereas a count cap retains the measured reduction. Attacker capability therefore supplies a concrete boundary for reusing a protection estimate.
