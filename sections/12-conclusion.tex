\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK organizes defense evidence around the deployment change for which it will be reused. The complete record audit exposes within-family object substitutions; configuration-linked joins and the six-axis holdout map identify the evidence that remains applicable. Controlled studies attach inference, state, utility and cost consequences to selected changes, including local calibration variability and timing-aware evaluation. The resulting decision table specifies which measurement is needed before carrying a defense claim to a new operating point.
