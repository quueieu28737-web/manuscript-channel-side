\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK turns dispersed defense evidence into traceable comparisons, configuration-bound outcomes and deployment measurement requirements. Its three contributions---record-level comparison, configuration-level joint binding and deployment decisions---connect each reuse claim to its supporting observations. The complete pair audit exposes changes of primitive, target and cost within shared family names. Configuration-linked joins establish which protection, utility and cost outcomes can be interpreted together, retaining the distinction between two records and one shared evidence package. The six-axis holdout synthesis separates attack generalization, local defended outcomes and transported defense calibration before connecting evidence to a deployment change. A positive cache-reuse example shows how to retain a candidate and its bound quality/cost package while identifying the specific measurement needed for another candidate. Published query membership, source-linked reasons and family/status sensitivity make these outputs inspectable before a defense claim is carried into a new deployment.

External retrospective cases and seven internal judgments illustrate the framework's use: retain a successful scoped result, separate a mismatched evidence package, or specify the additional deployment measurement. Distinguishing supporting counterexamples from a missing source premise and an unestablished defense candidate keeps each decision tied to its evidence. This traceable path from published outcomes to concrete measurement requirements is the framework's practical contribution.
