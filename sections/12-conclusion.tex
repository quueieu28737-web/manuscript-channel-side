\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Defense evidence transfers only across changes that preserve its protection and evaluation contract. Source-bound records and the audit make those conditions inspectable; experiments expose emitter, state, calibration and timing boundaries. Two-endpoint matrices turn model sensitivity into directional, target-specific tests. The practical decision procedure is to identify the changed boundary, retain matched inference, utility and cost objects, and re-measure that boundary before carrying a result forward.
