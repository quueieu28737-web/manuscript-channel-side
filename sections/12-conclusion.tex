\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Defense evidence can be reused only with its evaluated object and operating point intact. This SoK makes that requirement inspectable: the complete record audit exposes within-family substitutions, configuration-linked joins identify which outcomes belong together, and the six-axis holdout map distinguishes the deployment changes actually evaluated. The resulting procedure and decision table turn source-bound literature evidence into a concrete reuse judgment and a specific requirement for the next measurement.
