\section{Frozen R47 Source Map and Remaining Limits}\label{sec:r47-frozen-map}
Acquisition and screening are frozen for this revision; the freeze does not retroactively complete the protocol. The discovery interval remains January~1, 2018 through October~30, 2026, 13:15:46 UTC, with earlier foundations as background. Notes recorded later do not themselves establish availability at that cutoff. The extension is retrospective, nonblind and model-assisted, with prior results and reviews visible; no preregistration or independent human coding is claimed.

\paragraph{Separate eligibility from evidence type and goal}
Direct defenses (D) explicitly address auxiliary-observation confidentiality or unlinkability in LLM inference, serving or agent execution. Designs, formal analyses and implementations can qualify without an empirical attack-success score. Relevant generic hardware, ORAM, constant-time and traffic mechanisms remain M unless the source explicitly analyzes that setting. Semantic-only privacy, general model safety and secondary sources without the relevant auxiliary-observation claim remain A; unresolved cases stay pending. User/task privacy, model/service secrets, attribution and audit/detection integrity are separate protected interests. A defense of audit randomness therefore does not supply a user-privacy success or failure sample.

The original C5 empirical inference-evaluation condition, 82-family map, 19-family privacy/attribution sample and historical record codes remain intact. The six-source defense-first challenge also retains its prior purposive scope. R47 does not redefine either set or supply a finalized D-family denominator. Version-traceable papers, study families, retrieval routes and configurations are distinct units; repeated routes or paper versions do not become independent studies. Formal statements, proposed mechanisms and measurements retain their respective assumptions and limitations.

\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02522v1}{Opal v1} has a recorded source-version D judgment for personal-AI memory access outside trusted enclaves. Its stated privacy/integrity theorem is conditional on a public leakage function, enclave/cryptographic assumptions, a durable client counter and no ORAM stash overflow. Operation type and public trace shape remain visible; enclave-internal side channels are excluded. Ten-run latency and annual infrastructure-cost extrapolation are distinct observations, not paired residual-attack, task-quality and cost measurements. Proof correctness, publication identity and family relationships remain pending.

The \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22935v1}{eFPGA LLM design v1} also has a recorded source-version D judgment. It proposes equalization, masking, perturbation, execution shuffling and constant-shape traffic for separately described observers and assets. Qualitative comparison marks are neither measured protection nor an end-to-end theorem. Other integrity and supply-chain goals remain separate. Final family inclusion and independent semantic review remain pending. These deliberately selected sources illustrate the broader rule; they do not form a complete or deduplicated defense sample.

\paragraph{OTRO: conditional security and unreconciled costs}
The retained \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17358v1}{v1 notes} preserve the equal-access-count DRAM-trace theorem conditions, public length/count observations and excluded implementation channels. The uniform 20,000-prompt closed-world exercise is not a universal leakage bound. Plotted TTFT excludes access-count padding. The abstract's at-most-4.5\% and body average of 4.59\% remain unreconciled. The stated 2.8\,ms padding estimate divided by the named 116\,ms rebuild latency is about 2.4\%, whereas the text says less than 0.4\%; this arithmetic check is not a corrected measured result. Pool/memory text conflicts, figures, proof/implementation correspondence and padding-inclusive multi-client costs remain pending. The separately recorded v3 observations are not substituted for v1's algorithms, leakage or measurements.

\paragraph{Time Will Tell: ideal observation and selected tasks}
The retained \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2412.15431v1}{v1 follow-up} limits the 60-word prompt experiment to three selected high-bias tasks, unchanged three-shot examples and perfect output-token-count observation. Reported mean ASR changes from 88.2\% to 61.9\% for GPT-4o and from 79.3\% to 80.3\% for Gemma2-9b-IT; the former difference is 26.3 percentage points. Residual inference remains. The remote attack setup and separate model/language padding estimates do not establish a paired adapted-defense, quality and cost evaluation for the prompt intervention.

\paragraph{Frozen limitations}
Venue/index/citation coverage, uncollected tails, inaccessible sources, final eligibility, cutoff availability and unresolved publication/version/family identities remain limitations. A pass over saved program positions is not complete proceedings coverage, unique-paper accounting or full-text review. Title-only entries, secondary abstracts, project descriptions and separate theses retain their recorded scope. Source bytes and figures were not always saved or independently checked. Local hash/pointer validation checks bookkeeping, not source semantics or proof correctness. No missing observation is recast as defense failure, and no source-version D judgment implies completed independent review.

The R47 frozen-source-map revision added no experiments: the R46 replay, calibration, operating-cost and retail-sandbox results remained unchanged. Subsequent R49 analyses are reported in Section~\ref{app:r49-checks}. Historical statistics are preserved, while the revised prose makes their configuration-level and selection-conditioned interpretation explicit.
