\section{External Retrospective and Time-Ordered Internal Checks}\label{sec:r28-details}
\subsection{B: retrospective design and complete frame}
The external frame includes both defenses in Deep Fingerprinting, all three implementations in WFDefProxy, and all nine defenses in Mathews et al.'s Table II (Standard mode). DynaFlow's live prototype is separate because its execution, workload and cost objects differ: 15 records, 14 defense--study pairs, three follow-up studies. The 82-family/39-record LLM/agent audit is unchanged.

The protocol preceded ledger assembly after abstracts and search previews had exposed outcomes. Extraction is retrospective and outcome-informed; the proposed independent source-only/follow-up-only blind prediction study was not performed. We report neither prediction accuracy nor a random-item significance test. Source URLs, version/hash receipts, locators, inclusion rules and comparator outputs are archived. Papers are not redistributed.

Mechanism-name carryover omits measurements at changed objects; an always-failure rule wrongly discards the favorable B02/B03/B04/B11 controls. The audit routes 11 records to protected-fit/view evaluation and four to execution/cost measurement, matching established WF practice. This checks evidence routing, not predictive superiority. Different accuracy/TPR populations and cost denominators remain separate; Random-WT is not symmetric Walkie-Talkie. Additional security modes and layered configurations are not counted as independent events.
