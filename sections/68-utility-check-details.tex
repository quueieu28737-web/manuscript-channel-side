\subsection{B: retrospective design and complete frame}
The external frame includes both defenses in Deep Fingerprinting, all three implementations in WFDefProxy, and all nine defenses in Mathews et al.'s Table II (Standard mode). DynaFlow's live prototype is separate because its execution, workload and cost objects differ: 15 records, 14 defense--study pairs, three follow-up studies. The 82-family/39-record LLM/agent audit is unchanged.

The protocol preceded ledger assembly after abstracts and search previews had exposed outcomes. Extraction is retrospective and outcome-informed. We report neither prediction accuracy nor a random-item significance test. Source URLs, version/hash receipts, locators, inclusion rules and comparator outputs are archived. Papers are not redistributed.

Mechanism-name carryover omits measurements at changed objects; an always-failure rule wrongly discards the favorable B02/B03/B04/B11 controls. The 11 protected-fit/view requirements are B01--B02 and B06--B14; the four execution/cost requirements are B03--B05 and B15. The four favorable controls B02, B03, B04 and B11 overlap those categories. This routing matches established WF practice. These are worked examples of evidence routing. Different accuracy/TPR populations and cost denominators remain separate; Random-WT is not symmetric Walkie-Talkie. Additional security modes and layered configurations are not counted as independent events.
