\section{Validation, Limitations and Ethics}\label{sec:agenda}
\subsection{What has and has not been validated}\label{sec:validation}
The paper establishes two things. The source examples show that each boundary occurs in published LLM and agent defenses, and the two cases show that ignoring the attacker or affected-state boundary can reverse a conclusion by a large margin. It does not establish that analysts who use the card make better reuse decisions than analysts who use existing guidance, or that different analysts fill the card consistently. All cards in this paper were filled retrospectively by the authors with model assistance and with the source outcomes visible.

A study that tests both questions would proceed as follows. Recruit analysts with security-evaluation experience and randomly assign each to the card or to established guidance \cite{carlini2019evaluating,mathews2023critical}, with counterbalanced case order and a common time budget. Use reuse scenarios built from sources with known join errors, such as the Early Bird overhead, CachePrune \S5.7 and DP-MemView's exposed score, mixed with scenarios where reuse is sound. Score decisions against a reference adjudicated in advance by assessors blind to condition, and report decision accuracy, inter-coder agreement (for example, Cohen's $\kappa$ per field), completion time and their uncertainty, with participant and case effects modeled. Pilot the rubric and register the protocol before collecting outcomes. Until such a study is run, the card should be read as a structured proposal grounded in existing principles.

\subsection{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}\label{sec:validity}
\paragraph{Sources}
The source examples were selected purposively, starting from attack papers that met five inclusion conditions and extended by a small defense-first search (Appendix~\ref{app:sources}). They illustrate the boundaries and support no claims about how often the field reports any field of the card. Source coding was neither preregistered nor independently double-coded. Statements that a measurement was ``not located'' refer to the inspected version and sections. Several sources are preprints whose cited version differs from the latest one; Appendix~\ref{app:sources} lists the version questions that affect interpretation.

\paragraph{Case A}
The corpus is synthetic (100 topics, five templates), the task is binary, the transport is TLS loopback rather than a WAN, and the defense is applied to saved outputs rather than during generation. The fixed-logistic comparison and the additional splits were defined after earlier results on the same data were visible; their intervals hold fitted predictors fixed and omit model-selection uncertainty. A 500-session repeat was collected in a new runtime after an incomplete original run.

\paragraph{Case B}
The planner is a mutable service alias, the 98 tasks are a nonrandom convenience sample, and only one state-changing write is tested in one domain. There is no side-effect-free write control, and the recovery of the original run changed concurrency and transport, leaving ten requests with unknown outcomes.

\subsection{Ethics}\label{sec:ethics}
The source analysis uses public research papers. The cases use synthetic topics, cloned benchmark databases, simulated users and local sockets; no production accounts were modified and no human subjects were involved. Describing which observation a defense leaves uncovered can help an adversary choose a surface, so the artifact omits raw traces, credentials and operational detail while retaining source locators, derived labels, protocols, code and aggregate results. Redistribution of third-party papers and traces is subject to their terms.

\subsection{Use of generative AI and reproducibility}\label{sec:ai-use}
AI tools assisted research preparation, implementation, source reconciliation and prose. OpenAI GPT-6 Codex assisted analysis code, citation-inventory preparation and prose in an earlier revision; the complete earlier assistant history is not recoverable. Anthropic Claude assisted the present restructuring of the manuscript and an automated check of reference metadata. Model assistance extends to source coding: cards were drafted with model help, and the authors are responsible for every code and interpretation. Because no independent coder checked them, each evidence cell carries a locator so that readers can verify it directly.

On 4 November 2026 we checked every reference that carries an arXiv identifier against the arXiv API: all 80 matched in title and first author. Of 29 references with a DOI, 27 resolved through Crossref with matching titles; two DOIs cited only in the supplement did not resolve and are recorded in the artifact's citation checks. Case~A uses pinned local model artifacts; Case~B used a mutable alias, so a fresh run can reproduce the protocol but not the model. Archived inputs, saved predictions, configuration hashes and analysis code allow the reported statistics to be recomputed without new model calls.
