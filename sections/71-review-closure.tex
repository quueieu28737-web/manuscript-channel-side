Projection combines records at a shared configuration, a family/measurement mode, or a whole family. An edge is unanimous only if all original judgments agree; mixed edges retain all reasons. Original within-unit edges are hidden rather than resolved. This sensitivity projects the existing judgments without rereading or changing them. A random regrouping cannot calibrate correctness because the original 608 cross-question pairs were not adjudicated. Leave-family-out percentages in S33 quantify record weighting only.
\subsection{Reconstruction and evidence status}
The released data distinguish bibliographic publication status from the exact inspected source version, execution/measurement mode, author-assigned binding and computational consistency. A published record can still lack a requested deployment binding; a preprint can report a precisely located measurement whose scientific replication remains unestablished. Missing reports remain unavailable evidence for that request, not inferred omissions of experiments. The atlas does not assign a scalar paper-quality score.

All positive joins, all 33 mitigation records' query memberships and every family inference/cost crosswalk now appear in the submission PDF. The supplementary cell rationales and pair CSV allow deeper inspection. The new reporting-card template is accompanied by the filled Early Bird, TDXRay and CachePrune examples in the main text.
\subsection{Scope of the applications}
The external 15-record, three-study WF frame is a retrospective illustration of established evaluation practice. The internal judgments are author-designed examples; P5's source-premise failure gives no favorable evidence for the framework. The implementation case study is compressed in Appendix E, with its full statistics retained in S11--S32. Neither a nonsignificant contrast nor a time-ordered protocol establishes procedure accuracy.
