\subsection{External worked applications: retain, separate, remeasure}\label{sec:utility-b}
We apply the procedure to a fixed external frame: both defenses in Deep Fingerprinting, all three in WFDefProxy, and all nine in Mathews et al.'s reevaluation, with DynaFlow's live implementation kept as a separate record \cite{sirinam2018deep,gong2021wfdefproxy,mathews2023critical}. The resulting 15 measurement records represent 14 defense--study pairs in three studies. The ledger retains favorable outcomes alongside weaknesses, fixes the Standard setting for the nine-defense table, and records a locator and a concrete measurement decision for every row (S35).

The worked output separates decisions that a mechanism name conflates (Table~\ref{tab:r28-external}). Stronger protected-data fitting requires revisiting WTF-PAD's attack result, while Walkie-Talkie's symmetric-pairing result remains a valid scoped outcome. FRONT's live protection improves even as its bandwidth cost changes. A record-level audit therefore preserves the security observation and replaces the cost estimate separately. DynaFlow's live result needs a separate execution/workload record, preventing a simulated cost from being attached to a live attack result.

The 11 protected-fit/view requirements cover Deep Fingerprinting B01--B02 and Mathews et al.'s B06--B14; the four execution/cost requirements cover WFDefProxy B03--B05 and Mathews et al.'s live DynaFlow B15. Four favorable controls overlap these groups: symmetric Walkie-Talkie B02 and one-split TrafficSliver B11 in the first, live FRONT B03 and Tamaraw B04 in the second. The actionable output is a scoped retain, separate or remeasure decision for each record. The examples faithfully encode established WF practice. Outcomes were visible during extraction, so agreement is an illustration of the representation rather than evidence of prediction accuracy or an improvement over ordinary expert reading. These three WF studies do not establish LLM/agent effectiveness; the cache and tool examples provide the domain-specific application.
