The actionable output is a scoped retain, separate or remeasure decision for each record. The examples faithfully encode established WF practice. Outcomes were visible during extraction, so agreement is an illustration of the representation rather than evidence of prediction accuracy or an improvement over ordinary expert reading. These three WF studies do not establish LLM/agent effectiveness; the cache and tool examples provide the domain-specific application.
