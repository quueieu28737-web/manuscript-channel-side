\section{Complete Record-Local Audit and Joint Bindings}\label{app:record-joins}
This retrospective reanalysis applies R1--R9 to each of the 39 archived result records, yielding 351 cells. It retains the original source locators and hashes; source follow-up resolves the SparLeak utility correction and distinguishes CachePrune's shared default configuration and OTRO's padded/unpadded costs. The table below binds each judgment to a source locator and rationale.

\paragraph{Counting rule}
For a requested join, a mitigation record is applicable if none of the required items is inapplicable. A join is bound only if every required item is B and the evidence describes the same intervention/workload configuration. U (unreported), ? (unverified) and P (partial) are not bound; they remain distinct in the cell table. The six contextual controls are excluded from all mitigation join denominators. No family-level union of cells is used. Configuration-linked evidence need not use identical sampled requests and does not license a paired effect.

\paragraph{Positive joins and deduplication}
Inference plus utility: AHB online execution, CachePrune direct recovery, CachePrune contextual recovery and SparLeak replacement (4/22 records, three families/configurations). Inference plus cost: speculative REST padding, Dangol capture, Dangol live binding, and the two CachePrune records (5/22, three families, four configurations). Matched protected fitting/adaptation plus inference: UI offline and Dangol capture (2/17). Inference--utility--cost: the two CachePrune records share one default configuration's quality/cost package (2/20, one family/configuration). All five items together: 0/14. These query-specific counts describe the inspected collection. The 2/20 row is supported by CachePrune alone; two records reuse one configuration package and provide no estimate of the prevalence of such evidence across the literature.

\paragraph{Cost-object discipline}
The speculative REST record binds token-payload overhead, not verified live wire bytes; Dangol capture binds transformed capture cost, while its live record binds loopback wire/latency. CachePrune's secure default setting links recovery with generation quality and serving cost; its privacy-disabled cache-hit comparison is not borrowed. SparLeak's attack overhead is not a defense-cost observation. OTRO's unpadded TTFT does not price its separately derived epoch padding. These distinctions explain why cost cannot be copied by family name.
