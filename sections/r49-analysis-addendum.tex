\section{Post-hoc E4 and R44 Checks}\label{app:r49-checks}
These checks were performed after the original results were visible and are not independent replications. They use the preserved E4 capture records and original R44 episode receipts; no existing observation was overwritten.

\paragraph{Fixed-learner E4 comparison}
For the Qwen~8B four-chunk point, the saved topic-held-out predictions contain 500 sessions on 100 topics. Logistic regression was selected retrospectively as one common learner. The paired topic bootstrap samples 50 topics within each binary class, retains each sampled topic's five templates, and uses the same sampled topics for both conditions. Its 2,000 draws use seed 20261102 and hold all previously fitted predictors fixed. Intervals therefore omit model-fitting, calibration and post-selection uncertainty.

Five additional five-fold splits use seeds 11, 29, 47, 71 and 101, with topic-level stratification and no shared topics between training and evaluation within a split. Each fold independently fits the same logistic-regression pipeline on raw or defended training captures and scores the same defended test captures. A further single stratified 80/20 topic split, with the same learner fixed before its evaluation, gives D0 BA/AUROC 0.500/0.758 and D2 0.760/0.807 on the 20 held-out topics. No winner was selected on this holdout, but it is drawn from the previously inspected corpus and is not independent of the overall research history. The existing data, model choice and seeds were selected after earlier results were visible. Twenty-one capture uses route through previously verified recovery records; SHA-256 is rechecked for each selected capture. These are held-out evaluations of an already inspected dataset, not independent external evidence.

\paragraph{Local event-cap variability}
For each of three targets and five original topic folds, the 80 local training topics are resampled within class 500 times, retaining all five templates of each selected topic. Each draw recomputes the p95 event-count parameter using the original policy's interpolation and ceiling rule. The original 15 local caps independently agree with the frozen transport plan, and hashes and content digests of the 1,500 native streams were checked. These distributions describe fitted parameter variability only; no new defense replay or attack evaluation under the resampled caps is implied.

As a narrow overlap check for Qwen~8B fold~0, the 90-event cap imported from Qwen~0.6B also appears in 3 of the 500 local topic resamples. In the already saved captures for that fold, fixed-logistic defended-fit BA is 0.76 at both the 87-event local cap and the 90-event imported cap on the same 20 test topics. This single post hoc comparison does not evaluate the other locally resampled caps and does not establish equal protection or a causal source effect.

\paragraph{R44 recorded failure markers}
In the original mutable-alias convenience sample, 115 of 187 write-arm episodes failed. An automatic retrospective marker requires a cover write that changes the selected order's eligibility followed later by a planner call on that same order returning the native non-pending-order error. Twenty-eight failures satisfy this exact marker; 87 do not. Eighty-six of the 115 failures had a successful paired baseline. These sets overlap. The marker does not assign causality, and absence of the exact error cannot exclude quota effects; planner deviation and other causes were not independently adjudicated. The original 561 episode files were checked against their saved verification receipt.

\paragraph{Bibliographic checks}
Offline inventories found 43 main-paper and 108 supplement citation keys in the local BibTeX files. The 2 November 2026 check retained matching normalized titles for 66 of 71 cited arXiv entries; three responses were discarded and two returned tool errors. A 3 November targeted follow-up inspected versioned arXiv renderings for those five: their identifiers, titles and abbreviated author names match their BibTeX entries. Two of the five were checked in arXiv HTML after the abstract pages returned cache errors. Thus all 71 have a title-metadata check, not a full semantic or historical-byte audit. The Shape and Substance v1 HTML header displays 26 March 2026, while its manuscript-received note says 24 August 2026; the discrepancy is unresolved. Rendered SICC v1 and Turing Test on Screen v1 metadata likewise give submission months inconsistent with their identifier prefixes. Cached renderings do not authenticate inherited access dates, publisher versions or archived snapshots.
