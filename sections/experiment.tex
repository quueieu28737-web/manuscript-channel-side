\section{Controlled Measurements at the Two Boundaries}\label{sec:experiment}
The source map shows what the literature has not measured. Here we measure two of those gaps directly. The cover study binds recognition, target-task completion, whole-state integrity and cost to the same executions; it supplies Finding~3. The forwarding pilot tests the feedback path and calibrates how such tests should be run. The two studies keep separate populations and inferential results.
