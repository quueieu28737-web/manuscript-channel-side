\section{What a Result Can Support}\label{sec:scope}
In the original empirical ledger, one result record describes an evaluated target, receiver, intervention and outcome package. It is a comparison unit, not an independent trial. Changes of primitive, execution mode, target/receiver contract or separately measured cost object require distinct records; model, workload and parameter choices remain coordinates within a record. Numerical joins preserve those coordinates. A source-described common configuration can link separate evaluations without establishing identical requests or paired runs. The card retains the strength of that linkage, as well as observed mismatches between measurement objects.

A comparison is compatible for the requested object when required fields align, incompatible when an observed object conflicts, and unresolved when the necessary binding is missing. A token-recovery shortlist does not require equal costs; a cost comparison does. The source-level record identity, rather than a family name, determines which comparison is permitted.

The 39-record ledger contains 31 privacy/attribution mitigation records, two AHB evasion records and six contextual controls. The historical 20-family/33-record mitigation inventory includes AHB; the 19-family/31-record privacy/attribution inventory excludes that one family and its two records. The six controls are outside both mitigation inventories. These are nested accounting frames, not competing totals. The six-source defense-first challenge remains separate because its receivers and sampling frame differ. The broader frozen R47 map uses version-traceable papers, with family and configuration relationships recorded separately. Formal statements, designs and empirical results remain distinct evidence objects; pending identities and eligibility are not folded into the historical ledger. Record splits encode the distinctions the audit asks readers to preserve, so split counts and different-object pair counts partly follow from the construction. They are bookkeeping for inspecting individual joins, not independent empirical findings about the literature or a validation of the framework's effectiveness. The supplemental audit trail retains the complete record indices, granularity checks and query reconstruction.
