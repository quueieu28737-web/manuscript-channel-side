\subsection{Deployment and reporting implications}\label{sec:deployment}
Bind each result to its receiving view. Access-network operators, VPC monitors and receiving tools expose different observations; remote paths can reveal destinations even with Encrypted ClientHello \cite{rfc9849}. Ingress evidence does not automatically cover host instrumentation or downstream logs.

Bind state effects to the executed intervention. Read queries, isolated execution and gateway cover have different contracts. Dry-run, sandboxes and rollback have limits \cite{kubernetesdryrun,stripesandboxes,postgresrollback}; idempotence alone is insufficient, and sequence changes can survive rollback \cite{postgressequences}. Record the trust boundary, authorization, quotas and protected outcome with each result.

\paragraph{Unmeasured validity}
Analyst benefit remains untested. Assessing it requires a controlled comparison with existing guidance and independently assessed judgments. Stronger deployment claims need fixed models, a justified cohort, additional domains and a side-effect-free write control for R44. E4 needs independent or nested attacker selection and a local calibration-variability control.

\subsection{Ethics and artifact scope}\label{sec:ethics}
The audit uses public research sources. Evasion and research-intent privacy have different authorization relations \cite{zhu2026turing,jeong2026wra}; published attack methods may enable inference. The case study uses cloned benchmark databases, simulated users and controlled sockets, without modifying production accounts. Reconstruction materials include source locators, derived labels, protocols, code and aggregate results. Privacy, data-rights and service-term decisions for traces and third-party material remain unresolved; public access does not grant redistribution permission.

\paragraph{Generative AI use}
AI tools supported research preparation, implementation, source reconciliation and prose. Authors remain responsible for the audit codes and interpretations. Generative models supplied the studied streams and simulated conversations. Reconstructing analyses requires the specified archived inputs, model/trace identities, code and configuration hashes, environment and saved predictions; replay is not new task-utility evidence. Fresh execution also needs the stated weights or qualified mutable alias, service access and cloned sandbox state. Protocols retain hardware, budgets and recovery records. Missing access or redistribution rights limits reproducibility.
