\section{Synthesis and Research Agenda}\label{sec:agenda}

\subsection{Source dependencies of the two findings}\label{sec:sensitivity}
The transfer matrix separates established conditions from evidence for their Agent application. Model- and tool-side task observations draw on several studies; the decoy/utility mismatch comes from WRA's published text. Live OHTTP refitting and each composition, topology or scaffold transfer axis depend on individual preprints. The two located adapted privacy settings share one source. These dependencies matter more than treating every setting as an independent confirmation.

Source withdrawal tracks thirty-three statements across seven comparisons. Supplement S2 gives the complete summary and twenty removal scenarios, including individual sources, shared-author groups and publication filtering.

The strict published-text subset retains WRA and CHeaT: query recovery, the decoy/visibility distinction and a defined prevention outcome survive. Other label, population and policy contrasts depend on the wider corpus. Removing one transfer source removes evidence for that axis, rather than changing the other axes. The empirical feedback intervention supplies a separately identified controlled case; it does not turn source-dependent findings into replications.

\section{Validity, Ethics and Availability}\label{sec:validity}
\paragraph{Evidence scope}
Source locators and withdrawal analysis expose dependencies; index failures and query misses limit coverage. Extraction is author-performed with AI assistance, without independent human coding. The source-specific measurements and our controlled tasks represent different populations.

The paired mechanism study covers six synthetic actions with deliberately category-bearing supplementary records on one host. All adjusted primary tests exceed .05. The forwarding intervention changes content, encoded length and execution order together; equal tool-call counts do not isolate the first two. Complete-execution and overlap-threshold contrasts are reported alongside the focal phase results. Session resampling, including LR refitting, measures variation within this workload and selected learner family. Recognition receives phase or task windows; localization receives session boundaries and synthetic live background traffic. These conditions leave generalization to other workloads, enterprise background and gateways open. The remote retest changes the backend and network together and uses an alias without pinned weights.

Earlier serial, common-prefix and fixed-composition studies are archived as separate populations in the review artifact, with their failures, inconclusive results and unknown responses. Their outcomes are not pooled with the present experiment.

\paragraph{Ethics and assistance}
Inputs are synthetic, business actions update isolated fixtures, and capture filters restrict collection to the experiment. Remote model use has the account holder's authorization; provider-specific research permission and applicable terms remain unverified. Known failures and unknown responses are retained separately. No human-participant experiment, customer operation or hosted-provider vulnerability finding enters the study. AI tools assisted source extraction, implementation and writing; saved observations and executable analyses support the reported measurements.

Traffic inference can enable unwanted profiling as well as monitoring. The review package therefore contains synthetic, payload-free metadata, features, predictions and offline analyses. Credentials, private transcripts, raw captures and third-party full texts are excluded. Reusable analysis methods still carry misuse potential. A protection summary should report adapted attacks, remaining views and original-task outcomes together; a frozen attack's decline alone can overstate the measured protection.

\paragraph{Availability}
The accompanying review package connects the forty-four-setting evidence matrix, transfer relations, eligibility ledger, source locators and dependency calculations to the claims. Synthetic metadata, predictions, outcome markers, uncertainty scripts and manifests provide an offline verification route. Raw-to-metadata reconstruction needs the excluded captures; the mutable remote alias cannot reproduce fixed weights. The supplement presents the current study and source basis; the artifact also preserves the earlier experimental reports and all saved measurements. Compilable LaTeX and both PDFs accompany the package.

\section{Conclusion}
Classical traffic-analysis knowledge carries into Agent security through explicit access, observation and intervention conditions. The transfer matrix identifies two execution boundaries requiring additional checks: feedback crosses separately protected services, and executed cover changes application work. The fifteen-source evidence map ties these boundaries to actual observation rights and measurements. Among four query/workflow privacy settings, adaptation and original-task utility are located in different records; the published executed-decoy result leaves matched task utility open. The controlled feedback pilot retains its inconclusive adjusted comparisons and heterogeneous complete-execution outcomes. The practical priorities follow from the synthesis: evaluate every permitted residual view, and bind privacy, resource use and original-task outcomes to the same intervention.
