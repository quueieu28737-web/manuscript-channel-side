\subsection{Which conclusions depend on which sources}\label{sec:sensitivity}
The evidence supports different parts of the two-boundary account at different strengths. Published WRA links tool destinations to query recovery and separates executed-decoy recovery from the visibility condition's utility. Model-view measurements, live OHTTP refitting and the individual composition, topology and scaffold transfer axes depend on preprints. Multiple settings from one source retain that shared dependence.

Source withdrawal follows thirty-three statements across seven comparisons and twenty removal scenarios. In the strict published-text subset, WRA and CHeaT retain query recovery, the decoy/visibility distinction and a defined prevention outcome. Removing a transfer source removes evidence for that axis. The supplement gives the full accounting. The local cover study illustrates outcome coverage under an adverse control; the forwarding pilot supplies an unresolved causal comparison. Neither substitutes for missing source coverage.

\section{Validity, Ethics and Availability}\label{sec:validity}
\paragraph{Evidence scope}
The corpus combines query retrieval, selected seeds and citation tracing. Index failures and missed query terms limit coverage. Extraction is author-performed with AI assistance. Two dependent, non-blind AI recodings diagnose the manual; they provide no independent human reliability. The outcome disagreements led to source-specific corrections, with original codes and sensitivity retained. Source locators and withdrawal analysis make these dependencies inspectable.

The executed-cover case uses synthetic actions, local services, reused phrasings and supplied episode/call windows. Its intervals condition on fitted attacks and the chosen fixtures. Adverse writes are selected to exercise collateral effects, so the measured losses do not estimate production prevalence or WRA's research-decoy harms. Llama's baseline already has goal and state errors. Clearly labelled visible cover responses leave average endpoint effects unresolved. Business-state invariants, stronger attacks and other observation views require their own evidence.

The forwarding pilot uses deliberately category-bearing records and twenty nonempty test-session clusters. Its corrected primary comparisons remain inconclusive; content, length and execution order change together. Session resampling and LR refitting address variation within the selected workload and learner family. Live background traffic is synthetic. The hosted retest changes backend and network together and uses an unpinned alias. Supplement S1 reports these populations separately; earlier experiments and unknown responses remain in the artifact archive.

\paragraph{Ethics and assistance}
Inputs are synthetic, business actions update isolated fixtures, and capture filters restrict collection to the experiment. Remote model use has the account holder's authorization; provider-specific research permission and applicable terms remain unverified. Known failures and unknown responses are retained separately. No human-participant experiment, customer operation or hosted-provider vulnerability finding enters the study. AI tools assisted source extraction, implementation and writing; saved observations and executable analyses support the measurements.

Traffic inference can enable unwanted profiling as well as monitoring. The review package supplies extraction records, synthetic metadata, features, predictions and offline analyses. Synthetic planner records and fixtures permit state replay. Credentials, private transcripts, raw captures and third-party full texts are excluded; the remaining methods still carry misuse potential. A protection summary should present adapted attacks, permitted views and original-task outcomes together, since a frozen attack's decline can overstate protection.

\paragraph{Availability}
The accompanying package connects the forty-four-setting matrix, transfer relations, eligibility ledger and source locators to the claims. It includes the prior evidence archive, synthetic predictions and outcome records, uncertainty scripts and manifests. Excluded captures are needed for raw-to-metadata reconstruction, and a mutable remote alias cannot supply fixed-weight reproducibility. Compilable LaTeX and both PDFs accompany these materials.

\section{Conclusion}
Agent communication defenses operate across service and execution boundaries. The synthesis links each signal to an observer with specific access, and each cover policy to the work and state it can affect. Its concrete consequences are to evaluate every permitted residual view and to bind privacy, task outcomes and resource cost to the same intervention. The controlled study illustrates this joint record; the open agenda asks when feedback adds recoverable information and when practical cover satisfies a business contract. These questions preserve the Agent execution focus while making the supporting evidence and remaining tests explicit.
