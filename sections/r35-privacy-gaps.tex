Within the frozen corpus, the union of Type I and II contains 52 families; 19 have a scoped mitigation record. The remaining 33 families are enumerated below so that this coverage statement can be audited against the core map. This is a statement about the record-selection rules and inspected sources: several have contextual guardrails, noise, filters, decoy boundaries, or unbound claims. It does not assert that none of their papers or subsequent work evaluates any defense.
