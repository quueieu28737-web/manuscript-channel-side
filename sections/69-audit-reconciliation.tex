\subsection{One record ledger, one family projection}
The 39-record ledger and its 351 item judgments are the source of truth for the current family display. For each family/item, the projection returns B if any scoped record is B, otherwise P if any is P, then ?, U, A, and finally inapplicable. Thus B has an explicit record witness; P preserves known partial support; ? preserves a still-unverified record binding when no stronger support is available. The ordering summarizes available evidence, not overall study quality. Every record state remains available even when another record supplies a family's B. In particular, Dangol's stored-capture R5 witness does not resolve the live record's R5.

The projection changes 13 displayed family cells, without changing any record judgment or joined outcome (Table~\ref{tab:r32-reconciliation}). Earlier family displays mixed broader source descriptions with record-specific scopes. NetEcho and Early Bird therefore retain ? in R5 under the new projection, although the earlier source-scope reread classified their broader descriptions as partial. The six record-specific ? cells remain six; the current family summary has two. A bound cost in an unpadded or component record can appear in a family summary while remaining unavailable to a protected-inference join. The witness file lists all 180 family/item projections, their contributing record IDs, states and original source locators.

R6 now has 17 bound families among 20 applicable; R7 has 16 among 17. R7 is inapplicable for the three families represented here only by a structural/cost or component mitigation record: I Know What You Said, Remote Timing and TDXRay. The unchanged record-local joint results are 4/22 for inference with utility, 5/22 with cost, 2/17 for matched adaptation with inference, 2/20 for the triple join and 0/14 for all five bindings. The two triple-join records share CachePrune's one configuration package.

\subsection{Bibliography-stratified sensitivity}
The strata follow the frozen cited bibliography, retaining the consulted source version. Four mitigation families have a linked journal/conference publication: Early Bird (TIFS), I Know What You Said (USENIX Security 2025), TDXRay (S\&P 2026) and WRA (USENIX Security 2026) \cite{earlybird2025,gao2025iknow,hornetz2026tdxray,jeong2026wra}. Early Bird's inspected source is its author-posted v5, which records acceptance; venue linkage does not certify that every consulted PDF is the final typeset version. The remaining 16 families are cited through preprint/web versions. This partition does not assert that they have never appeared at a peer-reviewed venue.

Table~\ref{tab:r32-publication} applies the same five queries and applicability rules to both strata. Venue-linked families contribute five mitigation records, but only one or two records are eligible for each query. Their zero bound joins therefore cannot estimate a field-wide or venue-quality difference. The useful sensitivity result is more specific: the sole observed triple package depends on a cited preprint configuration, while the full ledger still identifies reusable component outcomes in both strata. Publication status is a provenance attribute, not a substitute for checking the configuration, receiver and cost object.

\subsection{Executable consistency contract}
Before compilation, the released generator rebuilds both summaries from the fixed ledger. Its checker verifies identities, counts, all 180 witnesses, five joint queries and 15 stratified cells; missing query rows fail. The witness file binds the record-table hash. Original source locators remain the basis of each judgment.
