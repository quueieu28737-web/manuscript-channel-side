\paragraph{Whole-ledger synthesis}
Each record has one primary measurement mode: 20 executed collections/prototypes, six trace transformations, five simulations/derived estimates, six component/calibration measurements and two unresolved placements (Table~\ref{tab:r9-record-index}). Eleven of the 20 mitigation families contribute more than one record, and nine of them span more than one mode; this is why a mechanism name cannot carry utility or cost between records. \subsection{Within-family and within-cohort record comparisons}
The completed audit covers all $\binom{39}{2}=741$ pairs. The 608 pairs outside the declared mechanism-question cohorts are not comparison failures. The 133 related pairs comprise 66 traffic-envelope, 45 cache-service, 15 local-memory, three behavioral-humanization, three generator-fingerprint and one fixed-instruction pair; they include all 15 same-family pairs. Three findings follow:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Same paper, different object.} Eight of the 15 same-family pairs substitute primitives, targets or cost objects, distributed across six of the 11 families with multiple records. CachePrune's direct recovery and contextual guessing differ despite sharing a defense configuration; OTRO's measured unpadded latency does not measure its epoch-padded operating point.
\item \textbf{Aligned outcome, unpaired population.} Of the other seven same-family pairs, four have different collection/execution populations and three lack a verified matched-test binding. Dangol's stored-capture ablation and live loopback remain distinct deployment measurements.
\item \textbf{Across records.} Of 133 related pairs, 17 support a source-qualified descriptive comparison, 105 have an established object/receiver/target mismatch, and 11 remain unresolved. Descriptive compatibility permits scoped comparison, not pooled effects or a ranking across heterogeneous costs.
\end{itemize}
The final inventory retains all 528 earlier pair judgments and adds 31 related judgments for the six expansion records, with source locators and reasons. Examples illustrate this census rather than define its sample. The 15 same-family pairs share records and families. Removing KVGov leaves five mismatches among 12 pairs; removing Whisper Leak leaves eight among 12. Across all 26 family deletions, related-pair compatibility ranges from 7.8\% to 15.2\%. Resolving all 11 uncertain pair judgments favorably would increase the compatible count from 17 to 28, with the 105 known mismatches unchanged. S33 reports each cohort and deletion. These are inventory sensitivities, not independent-sample prevalence estimates.

\paragraph{Why the record changes the reuse decision}
The census distinguishes three outcomes for a deployment query. A known object mismatch requires a new measurement at the intended operating point; an unresolved binding requires source reconciliation; a descriptive match permits a scoped comparison while retaining each cost's denominator. These outcomes prescribe different work. For example, aligning two packet-size accuracies cannot supply the missing online execution cost, and measuring a component's throughput cannot establish a planner's success under response deadlines. The 17 descriptive matches are useful precisely because their permissions remain explicit.

\paragraph{Which evidence packages are available}
Record-local joins resolve progressively stronger deployment queries: inference with utility is bound in 4 of 22 eligible records (three families/configurations), inference with cost in 5 of 22 (three families, four configurations), and matched adaptation with inference in 2 of 17. Their intersection cannot be reconstructed by adding these counts: only CachePrune's two outcomes share one inference--utility--cost package. A deployment analyst can therefore reuse the bound component outcome, price a missing cost, or measure workflow utility according to the actual join. This is more informative than treating every family-level B as a complete deployment result. Accepting P and ? as if bound leaves the triple join at two records, but changes matched adaptation--inference from two to 16 of 17. Thus uncertainty concentrates differently by query. Removing CachePrune removes the sole triple package; no other deletion does. These checks locate which additional source bindings would change a decision, rather than turn missing reports into evidence of failure.
