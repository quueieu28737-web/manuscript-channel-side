\subsection{The question is which configuration the evidence supports}
The C1--C5 attack-evaluation frame links 19 privacy/attribution families to 31 mitigation records, including attack-oriented and defense-oriented studies. Two AHB evasion records and six contextual controls remain outside this synthesis. These selected records support inspection of particular evidence packages; their reporting counts do not estimate how often defense research measures utility, cost or adaptive resistance.

R8 resolves the claimed function before asking for a quality score. Changed prompts, sampling, approximate computation or component coverage need matched quality evidence. Lossless framing, fixed-trace transformations and exact logical preservation instead support a narrow content/function claim. Delay, resources, timeouts and task completion remain separate outcomes for both classes.

\subsection{Cache reuse: what can support a candidate configuration?}\label{sec:cache-decisions}
CachePrune excludes key--value (KV) cache entries marked sensitive from cross-user sharing. Its v1 Table~1 reports QASPER direct exact recovery of 0\% and contextual exact recovery of 3.21\%. Table~2 reports default QASPER F1 of 0.28 versus 0.30 without sharing, and time to first token of 273 versus 797\,ms, using Mistral-7B on a single H100 \cite[3.1,5.1--5.2,5.6]{wu2026cacheprune}. The direct result is conditional on detector-marked spans and an abstract binary cache-reuse oracle, not measured WAN timing. Detector errors and contextual inference from other tokens remain possible. These source-reported values are descriptive. The default configuration supplies a useful association, but identical requests or paired runs across the protection and serving tables are not established. Its two recovery targets reuse this evidence rather than providing independent validation. The fixed-chunk comparison in Section~5.7 disables privacy filtering and cannot price the protected configuration. Retain the labeling assumptions and operating point when assessing feasibility; serving performance does not price every deployment resource.

Early Bird's minimum-shared-prefix rule instead uses measured hit/miss classifiers to parameterize a simulated oracle and a $K$-token predictor. Its v5 Table~VI reports token recovery of 91.5\% at $K=1$ and 49.0\% at $K=4$ \cite[V-A]{earlybird2025}. The approximately 6\,ms overhead in \S V-B belongs to a separate semantic-cache deidentification prototype, evaluated on 1,000 Presidio sentences. It cannot price the prefix rule. We did not locate deployed-serving cost or matched task utility for that rule in the inspected mitigation section. Both mechanisms can enter a threat-specific shortlist, but their recovery numbers are not a controlled comparison. The prefix candidate needs its own serving-cost and functional measurements.

Other source bindings preserve different functional objects: SparLeak links reconstruction and ROUGE to replacement intensity, while TDXRay measures vocabulary coverage \cite{chen2026sparleak,hornetz2026tdxray}. The supplement's \emph{Current-code reconstruction} retains their source witnesses and partial bindings.

For NetEcho/Whisper framing, SPEC aggregation and OTRO's token preservation, a structural witness supports only the stated content/function invariant \cite{netecho2025,whisperleak2025,wei2024speculation,lee2026otro}. SPEC and Dangol also combine scoped preservation with measured inference and cost, but payload, capture, loopback and serving costs answer different budget questions. None can substitute for an unmeasured deadline or task outcome.

\paragraph{Separate components and unresolved timing evidence}
SpliceDefense v1 reports nonzero signed residuals for constant-time boundary fusion (CTBF), including a bulk median of $-9$\,ms; their sign alone establishes neither timing indistinguishability nor a successful adaptive attack \cite[VII-C, Table~II]{spliceleak2026}. Quantized chunk padding (QCP)'s reported sub-1\% prefill cost concerns another component, not a paired CTBF or combined-deployment cost. The local simulated-tenant experiments assume logical co-residency, and the longer extraction cases deliberately include the true token in a 21-candidate set. Table~II's 15 tasks/75 steps and the separate ten-token targets remain unreconciled. Defended adaptive distributions, task quality and combined cost remain pending.

\subsection{Hardware cases expose another selection boundary}
SecEmb was already adjacent in the original map. It replaces embedding-table access with deep hash embedding and argues protection against its stated memory/control-flow observer. Its GPT-2 medium/OpenWebText results report minimum perplexity 15.0 versus 14.6 for lookup and generation overhead of 2--5\%, depending on batch size \cite[V-B,VI-D]{umar2025secemb}. The inspected PRIME+SCOPE attack demonstration instead uses DLRM; it cannot supply a GPT-2 recovery score. Exact checkpoint/run linkage of GPT-2 quality and latency also remains unverified. This is useful defense and functional evidence that an empirical-attack inclusion rule can leave outside the core sample.

Palermo v1 considers a trusted on-chip system and an external-memory/bus observer, using a simulated GPT-2/OpenORCA workload \cite[II-B,VI--VIII]{ye2024palermo}. Figure~9's mutual-information statistic concerns binary stash/tree state and median-split response timing; its near-zero value is not a prompt-recovery result or a general zero-leakage bound. Modeled execution performance and synthesized hardware costs are separate evidence. Output quality was not located in the inspected evaluation sections, and identical runs across the security and performance figures were not established. This case supports an observer-specific hardware tradeoff, subject to those limits. These selected examples neither complete hardware coverage nor estimate omissions; they leave the frozen ledger unchanged.

\paragraph{Retain a conditional guarantee at its stated boundary}
OTRO v1 states DRAM-trace indistinguishability for equal numbers of tokenizer table accesses under its ORAM and pseudorandom-function assumptions; computation timing and other implementation effects are excluded \cite[III,V--VII, Appendix~B]{lee2026otro}. Access count, or padded epoch count, and other public quantities remain visible. Its uniform 20,000-prompt closed-world leakage exercise is not a universal bound. Plotted TTFT excludes access-count padding, and the cost text contains unresolved discrepancies. These are separate objects, not a paired, padding-inclusive multi-client protection/quality/cost result. The frozen supplement also retains Opal's stated enclave/ORAM guarantee and an eFPGA LLM countermeasure design as source-version D judgments, with family identity and independent review pending. Their evidence types do not require a scored attack and do not imply end-to-end measured confidentiality.

\subsection{Agent-associated interventions need distinct task claims}
UI preserves saved action order; Dangol preserves fixed message bodies; WRA returns only the real output despite concurrent research decoys. These support narrow preservation claims. The cloud prompt/temperature interventions can change generation quality. None of these four map-selected families binds an explicit same-condition end-to-end task score, but they do not form a homogeneous sample of agent defenses. WRA is the sole added-call example in this subset; its usefulness judgment uses another condition. The actionable question is whether the particular added execution changes task completion, not whether an aggregate count establishes a field-wide utility gap \cite{uitraces2026,jeong2026wra,dangol2026workflow,pouryousef2026traffic}.

\subsection{What the selected defense-first sources add}
The challenge cards supply positive functional measurements and CacheSolidarity's scoped exact-prefix preservation. They differ from the original sample in selection, receivers and outcomes, so their quality counts are not a comparison of reporting rates. A functional measurement also need not form a joint protection/function/cost package (Table~\ref{tab:r42-defense-first}).

OCELOT links protection, task success and mediation cost at its semantic release boundary; its ledger invariant and calibrated inference guarantee remain distinct. AirGapAgent binds disclosure and answer utility for third-party contextual extraction. SICC explicitly isolates form-only inference, and DP-MemView separates its compliant semantic interface from an internal-score diagnostic. These observations require receiver-specific judgments. PlanTwin's unrestricted-field diagnostic cannot be joined to budgeted planning quality. The cards therefore guide different decisions even where functional measurements are available.
