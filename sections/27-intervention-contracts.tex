\subsection{Agent-associated interventions need distinct task claims}
Four map-selected families require distinct outcomes. UI preserves saved action order; Dangol preserves fixed message bodies; WRA returns only the real output despite concurrent research decoys. These support narrow preservation claims. The cloud prompt/temperature interventions remain generation-quality-applicable (0/1 B, 1/1 accepting P). None of the four binds an explicit same-condition end-to-end task score. WRA is the sole added-call example (0/1 matched task score); its usefulness judgment uses another condition. The 0/4 task inventory therefore spans different interventions \cite{uitraces2026,jeong2026wra,dangol2026workflow,pouryousef2026traffic}.

\subsection{Family-level inference and cost often describe different records}
Eight families have explicit inference and explicit cost somewhere in their evaluations. In five---Early Bird, SpliceLeak, Time Will Tell, KVGov and OTRO---no individual record carries both. The distinctions are concrete: prefix policy versus semantic processing; background-load mitigation versus QCP; a length instruction versus a padding estimate; partial flushing versus HMAC; and padded versus unpadded ORAM. SPEC, Dangol and CachePrune supply the five same-record inference/cost results listed in Table~\ref{tab:r35-positive}. \subsection{Execution evidence is distributed across measurement modes}
Among the 31 privacy/attribution records, 13 are executed collections/prototypes, five trace transformations, five simulations/derived estimates, six component/calibration measurements and two unresolved placements. The 12-record network-receiver subset contains six executed, four transformed and two unresolved records. These modes are a mixture rather than a predominance of network replay. An executed prototype still supplies only its measured local scope.

\paragraph{Comparison permissions as a supporting index}
The question groups index 133 pairs, of which 17 retain a scoped object match. For example, Early Bird and CachePrune inform a token-recovery shortlist while keeping their costs separate.
