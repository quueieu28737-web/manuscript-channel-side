\paragraph{Whole-ledger synthesis}
The complete census assigns 20 records to executed collection/prototypes, six to trace transformation, five to simulation/estimates, six to component/calibration measurements and two to unresolved placement (per-record assignments in the supplement). \subsection{Within-family and within-cohort record comparisons}
While the machine-readable audit retains all $\binom{33}{2}=528$ baseline pairs for completeness, 426 pairs cross unrelated mechanism cohorts and are outside scope by construction. The 13 within-family pairs are included in the 102 within-cohort pairs: $528=426+102$, with no additional subtraction of 13. Focusing on the \textbf{13 within-family pairs} (multiple records inside the same paper) and \textbf{102 within-cohort related pairs} (66 traffic-envelope, 28 cache-service, 3 behavioral-humanization, 3 generator-fingerprint, 1 local-memory, 1 fixed-instruction) reveals why paper-level comparisons fail:
\begin{itemize}
\item Even within the \textbf{13 same-family pairs} ( plus 2 same-family pairs in the additions: \texttt{CACHEPRUNE-selective} vs.\ \texttt{CACHEPRUNE-context} and \texttt{OTRO-unpadded} vs.\ \texttt{OTRO-epochpad}), nearly half (6/13 baseline; 7/15 full) measure different primitives, targets or cost objects (e.g., Early Bird's $K$-prefix oracle simulation vs.\ its GPTCache semantic prototype; SpliceDefense's CTBF signal suppression vs.\ QCP-only prefill cost; OTRO's unpadded TTFT vs.\ derived epoch-padding cost).
\item Among the seven aligned-outcome baseline same-family pairs, four evaluate different collection or execution populations (e.g., Dangol's stored-capture feature ablation at 0.17 accuracy vs.\ live loopback OHTTP at 0.30 accuracy) and three lack a verified matched-test binding, so none licenses cross-record subtraction as a single paired deployment effect.
\item Across the \textbf{102 related-cohort pairs}, only 16 (15.7\%) support a source-qualified descriptive comparison, whereas 80 (78.4\%) exhibit positively established receiver, target or cost-object mismatches and 6 (5.9\%) remain unresolved.
\end{itemize}
