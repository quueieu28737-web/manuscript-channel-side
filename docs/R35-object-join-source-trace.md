# A5: source and experiment trace

This is an evidence map for `sections/73-object-join-evidence.tex`. The examples are bounded applications of existing evaluation principles; they do not establish priority or novelty over website-fingerprinting or machine-learning robustness literature.

1. **Serving primitive and cost:** Early Bird, V-A–B, primary source https://arxiv.org/html/2409.20002v5 (around lines 378–395) reports an average 6 ms de-identification delay relative to about 0.14 s for an unprotected semantic-cache hit, giving approximately 4%. Its prefix-recovery/prefix-isolation experiment is a different primitive and outcome; no new causal or quantitative comparison of costs is implied. CachePrune source https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23640v1 §5.2–5.6 and Table 2 reports QASPER quality 0.28 vs 0.30 and time to first token 273 vs 797 ms, CachePrune vs no sharing. Quality is as defined by that source; these values do not rank Early Bird against CachePrune. Repository checks: `manuscript/sections/11-discussion.tex`, `manuscript/sections/66-source-reconciliation.tex`, and the record index.
2. **State changes:** `manuscript/sections/35-tau-cover-bridge.tex` and `manuscript/tables/tau-cover-bridge.tex` (`tab:tau-cover-bridge`) report 97 eligible tasks × 3 selection seeds = 291 units per position. The 173/291 is the before-insertion item-self-write mutation count, NOT 173 independent customers or tasks. Read-only and isolated-shadow equality is reference-relative database equality under the simulator and construction; it does not imply privacy or production safety. Subsequent positions have different figures. Source files relative to package root: `experiments/reviewer-audit/retail_units.json`, `retail_utility.json`, `retail_replay.py` and `recompute.py`; `35-tau-cover-bridge.tex` names the pinned setup and explains 118/291 failed writes.
3. **Live utility:** `manuscript/sections/55-live-planner-utility.tex` S26 and `manuscript/sections/62-appendix-case-study.tex` document 50 baseline and 50 prefix-shadow episodes, 37 and 33 successes; the -8.0 point paired estimate has customer-bootstrap CI [-23.9, 6.5] over 37 customers. The source is `experiments/sp2027-r15-live-planner/README.md` and its linked result/verification artifacts. Known tasks; mutable served GLM alias; generated new model decisions; 120 ms configured tool deadline and injected delay; no terminal flush, constant framing, WAN, or full joint defense. No statistically resolved decline. Do not claim the experiment measures full joint-defense utility or end-to-end WAN latency.
4. **Prior foundations:** Juarez et al. CCS 2014 https://mjuarezm.github.io/assets/pdf/critical-ccs14.pdf studies attacks under browsing, location and browser-version shifts. Mathews et al. SP 2023 https://oaklandsok.github.io/papers/mathews2023.pdf, abstract and §VI/VII, reevaluate nine defenses, use defense-specific attacks, and execute DynaFlow on-network. Tramèr et al. https://www.usenix.org/conference/scainet20/presentation/tramer; Carlini et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06705. The present examples specialize existing evaluation logic to distinct objects in the examined LLM/agent evidence, not a new information-processing theorem or entirely new adaptation/online-cost principle.
