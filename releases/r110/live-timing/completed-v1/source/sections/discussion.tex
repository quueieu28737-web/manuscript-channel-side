\section{Synthesis and Research Agenda}\label{sec:agenda}
\subsection{Which conclusions depend on which sources}\label{sec:sensitivity}
Source withdrawal follows 33 statements across seven comparisons and 20 removal scenarios (Supplement S2.4). With only published texts, WRA and CHeaT retain query recovery from tool destinations, the decoy/visibility utility mismatch behind Finding~2, and a defined prevention outcome. Finding~1's model-view half, live OHTTP refitting and each transfer axis depend on preprints. The controlled studies add implementation-specific measurements, including cover removal using record timing and the measured cost of a shadow planner; they do not replace missing source coverage.

\subsection{Lessons for defense designers and evaluators}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Name every retained view.} A tool-side or single-step defense leaves model-link, recipient and later-request views unless it is shown to cover them. Report an adapted attack on each permitted view, including single-link rules available to a joint observer.
\item \textbf{Report privacy and utility from the same intervention.} None of the four mapped query/workflow privacy settings documents both an adapted attacker and matched task utility. Utility must include whole-state correctness and resources, not only task completion.
\item \textbf{Test the cover schedule and its cost.} Immediate controller cover is removable without cover labels. Live historical waits leave an advantage for adapted context/decoding attacks; a shadow planner changes that comparison while approximately doubling model tokens. Report achieved observer timings, residual task inference and resources together.
\item \textbf{Calibrate against simple baselines.} Concurrency, position rules and constant-output detectors can rival learned attacks and costly shaping.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Validity, Ethics and Availability}\label{sec:validity}
\paragraph{Evidence scope}
Index failures and missed query terms limit coverage. An independent human coder reproduced every Finding~2 decision; only that subset, not every extracted field, has a reliability estimate. Section~\ref{sec:scope} states which fields the findings depend on.

The cover case uses synthetic actions, local services, repeated phrasings and supplied call windows. The older timing analyses are receipt-level counterfactuals; the five-arm study collects actual TLS records under a frozen plan on reused fixtures. It adds no unseen-workload confirmation or model-link measurement. Same-service calls serialize. Pools come from an earlier no-cover phase and caches persist, so achieved distributions and latency ratios include implementation and cache history. Intervals condition on fitted attacks and fixtures. Shadow equivalence concerns the validation-selected accuracy comparison, not universal role indistinguishability. Adverse writes test a state-checking boundary and do not estimate production prevalence.

The forwarding pilot uses category-bearing records and twenty nonempty test clusters. Projection changes content, length and execution order together, and its lessons are descriptive. The hosted retest (Supplement S1) changes backend and network together and uses an unpinned alias. It supports no main-text claim.

\paragraph{Ethics and assistance}
All inputs are synthetic, business actions update isolated fixtures, and capture filters restrict collection to the experiment. No human participants, customer operations or provider vulnerabilities are involved. The hosted retest used the account holder's authorization; provider-specific research terms remain unverified. AI tools assisted extraction, implementation and writing; saved observations and executable analyses support every measurement. Traffic inference can enable profiling as well as monitoring. We therefore release payload-free metadata, features, predictions and offline analyses, and exclude credentials, transcripts, raw captures and third-party full texts.

\paragraph{Availability}
The review package links the 44-setting matrix, transfer relations, eligibility ledger and source locators to each claim. It also includes synthetic planner records and fixtures for state replay, predictions, uncertainty scripts and manifests. Raw-to-metadata reconstruction needs the excluded captures.

\section{Conclusion}
Agent communication defenses have two execution boundaries: task information can travel across independently controlled services, and cover can perform business work. The evidence maps connect these boundaries to observation rights and matched outcomes. The technical case adds an operational consequence: a controller's dispatch schedule can identify its cover. Actual historical delays leave gains for adapted attacks; a second planner shifts validation's choice to size-only attacks at a substantial token cost, while task inference remains. Evaluating the schedule, every permitted residual view and whole-state outcomes together gives a concrete protection decision for the tested deployment.
