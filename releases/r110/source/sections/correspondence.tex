\subsection{Reading the transfer matrix}
Open-world prevalence, false alarms, adaptive attacks and workload shift are established concerns~\cite{juarez2014critical,hayes2016kfp,dyer2012peekaboo,mathews2023critical,arp2022dos,tramer2020adaptive}. Localization also has direct precedents: Wang and Goldberg study traffic splitting without reliable page boundaries; BAPM and ARES address mixed multi-tab traces~\cite{wang2016realistic,guan2021bapm,deng2023ares}. WF is neither inherently deterministic nor restricted to one link. Here these issues interact with feedback across independently controlled services and cover executed as application work.

\emph{Retained} means that an established evaluation obligation still applies; \emph{extended} identifies an additional Agent execution condition; \emph{access mismatch} prevents importing a result under the proposed observer; and \emph{untested} means that the mapped evidence leaves the specific performance claim open. These relationships are separate from the number and publication status of supporting sources. Distributed tracing illustrates access mismatch: WAP5 and vPath use host instrumentation~\cite{reynolds2006wap5,tak2009vpath}, which can label an experiment without becoming a passive attacker's input.

Five questions locate the resulting evidence: signal mechanism, supplied-unit recognition, named deployment transfer, stream localization and protection. They are distinct tests. A reported score binds a target to an observation and a tested deployment; Tables~\ref{tab:attack-map}--\ref{tab:attack-recipient} preserve those bindings across the field.

\subsection{Task boundaries and execution changes}
AgentPrint supplies browser interactions, WRA research traces, the communication-graph study workflows, the cloud benchmark execution timestamps, and ANT runtime-defined stages~\cite{agentprint2025,jeong2026wra,dangol2026workflow,pouryousef2026traffic,li2026ant}. ANT's 47-group primitive test scores 0.355--0.462 accuracy on 2,101 supplied segments. Its episode split separates parent executions, while the supplied boundaries define a recognition test. Event localization additionally requires recall and false events on proposed intervals.

PersonaFingerprint keeps one session's windows in one partition~\cite{song2026personafingerprint}. A neighboring published study classifies non-overlapping 5,000-packet windows without page markers, primarily testing human browsing with Agent-generated training traces~\cite{song2026redefining}. PINGPONG and voice-invocation work demonstrate detection against background traffic~\cite{trimananda2020pingpong,ahmed2023voice}.

Transfer names what changes while the target remains fixed: wording, session, composition, topology, scaffold or model. Reported composition, topology and scaffold losses concern different axes (Section~\ref{sec:targets}). Simultaneous Agent tasks and model waits also change how tool bursts relate to complete executions. Our live comparison separates supplied-window inference from localization and retains physical connection identities.
