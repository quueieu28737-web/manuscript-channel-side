The SoK maps 44 settings from 15 direct Agent/tool communication sources.
Its attack map distinguishes network observers from recipients and active
auditors, and records targets, features, learners, denominators and scores.
Its defense map connects mechanisms, costs, residual exposure and adaptation.
The mechanism layer retains 16 published communication studies and six model
serving studies; these are not counted as additional direct Agent evidence.

The fixed retrieval window is 1 January 2024 through 9 November 2026. The
review artifact includes the prior query/inclusion protocol, native arXiv and
web screening records, publication checks and one reference-tracing pass.
The search is bounded: database access failures and unresolved identities are
recorded. Four direct sources were added. Three works have verified formal
publication identities, but detailed claims in one extended preprint are not
upgraded to published-text evidence by its shorter workshop counterpart.

The supplement gives the current study, source locators and conclusion-specific
withdrawal summary: 33 premises, 20 scenarios, 140 rows. The complete 44-setting
extraction and indexed historical reports are in the review artifact.
The analysis distinguishes intent, logged behavior, execution success and
policy context, and reports which claims lose support when sources are removed.
Publication status and the scope of checks performed are separate attributes.

The review-artifact ZIP provides extracted evidence, screening ledgers,
packet-metadata-only inputs, saved predictions and offline checking/refitting
scripts. Protocol/deployment and defense-design maps connect visible signals,
trust boundaries, costs and practical protection choices. A running support-
ticket example distinguishes transit, recipient and active observation.

The randomized feedback study uses fixed Qwen3:8B and Llama3.1:8B models,
LangGraph, the official MCP SDK, TLS 1.3, HTTP/2 and streamed model output.
Full and Projected forwarding are paired on the original tool feedback.
Dedicated, Shared and Joint conditions compare connection and shaping policies.
Random session compositions include absent classes, repeated classes and empty
sessions. The supplied-window representation combines visible connections;
continuous localization separately scores distinct task instances. All declared
attackers and business outcomes are retained. No analyst study is performed.

The remote Full/Projected retest is a separate population using a mutable
hosted alias and local tunnel capture. Provider-specific research permission
and applicable service terms remain unverified. It is not a controlled
physical-NIC WAN performance benchmark. Earlier measurements and unknown
responses retain their original populations, including the inconclusive
earlier common-prefix study and the mostly unsuccessful 1B executor.

Third-party full texts, raw packet captures, transcripts, TLS keys, credentials
and participant answers are excluded from the delivered packages. Versioned
public source links and the extraction sheets provide the verification route.
This local delivery is not a published anonymous repository.

Two execution dependencies organize the synthesis: information can travel
through a feedback path across services, and cover can become application work.
The new intervention study tests the feedback path; executed-cover consequences
remain source-supported findings. REVIEWER-GUIDE.md in the review package links
claims to evidence. feedback-interventions/README.md describes the new study,
the sanitized local collection route and the portable offline checks. The
earlier live-concurrency/ directory remains a separate fixed-composition cohort.

R101 adds a bounded transfer matrix, a deployment/access table and descriptive
availability counts using the existing 44-setting corpus. Four query/workflow
privacy settings contain two located protected-condition adaptation results,
one matched original-task utility result and no setting containing both.
Identity obfuscation and adversarial-Agent blocking use separate objectives.
The complete checklist, protocol map and survey comparison remain in S5/S7.
No collection, fitting, hypothesis test, source search or analyst study was added.
The title, two Agent execution insights and all experimental values are retained.
The review package includes transfer-synthesis/ and verify_transfer_map.py.
