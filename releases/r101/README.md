R101 keeps the Agent communication side-channel SoK and its two insights. The
revision adds a transfer matrix, deployment/access mapping and question-specific
evidence counts from 15 direct studies, 44 settings and 22 selected mechanism
anchors. No new experiment, model fit, significance test or literature search
was performed for this revision.

Four query/workflow privacy settings from two studies contain two located
adaptation comparisons, one original-task utility measurement and no setting
containing both. The two adapted settings belong to one preprint; the utility
measurement is the published WRA visibility condition. Executed decoys are a
separate condition. Identity obfuscation and adversarial-Agent blocking retain
separate purposes and denominators.

All original experimental results are preserved, including all 12 primary
adjusted comparisons above .05. The main manuscript has 15 pages, with references
starting on page 13; the supplement has 23 pages. Both PDFs were checked page by
page, and the 53-file source ZIP independently rebuilt to identical page text,
rendered pixels and bibliographies. VERIFICATION.json records these checks.

Compile source/main.tex and source/supplement.tex separately using pdfLaTeX and
BibTeX, or upload latex-source.zip to Overleaf and choose the main document.
Use the versioned source folder or the clean ZIP for a reproducible build.

This public export contains the manuscript, source closure and extracted
evidence tables. The larger local review archive and private collection inputs
are not included. Third-party full texts, raw captures, transcripts, credentials
and participant responses are excluded. Statements in the frozen documents
about local availability describe their build-time status; this GitHub export
adds access to the specific subset listed here, not a complete experimental
artifact or an anonymous review repository.
