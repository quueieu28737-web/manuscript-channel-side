Removing time lowers recognition significantly in three of four history cells after correction within the eight-contrast timing family. Added record-context attacks improve over refitted baselines in five of eight cells, including all four history cells. Nonsignificant comparisons, original negative results, validation selections and descriptive test maxima are retained separately. Shadow-policy results do not establish online-planning superiority or role indistinguishability.

These are exploratory analyses of existing observations, not a new cohort. Papers are based on R117 commit b26b5bab8afae5031abf7a643d2c0b4e0cf49eeb. The reviewed analysis code was frozen before new scoring at 994e8e3b6dade9a544f4a2793128d01d0ce133dd. Earlier R117 draft code remains historical; the versioned R118 artifact identifies the executed revision and exact dependency bytes.

The public checker recomputes released aggregate arithmetic. Full refitting, per-fixture state replay, individual role/boundary verification and feature reconstruction require withheld local inputs. Per-example records, timestamps, predictions, fixtures, captures, keys and full private review packages remain LOCAL ONLY. No new collection, API/model request, analyst study or literature expansion occurred during this integration.
