\section{Reproduction scope and evidence archive}
The accompanying artifact separates executable implementations, aggregate arithmetic and retained individual observations. The live-study implementation and frozen training pools identify the five policies. Aggregate attack tables give paired discordance counts and family membership for inspecting McNemar and Holm arithmetic. The record-only scripts retain their original candidate grid and explicitly separate the later exploratory eventizer and context/joint-decoding extension.

The added release supplies aggregate CSV/JSON tables for every ablation and record-context candidate, all selected paired contrasts, actual-terminal strata, exact shadow label/role summaries and event audits. An arithmetic checker recomputes exact paired $p$ values and Holm correction from released discordant counts, and table-generation code links displayed counts to those inputs. A portable code-identity receipt gives the frozen implementation hashes and library versions without publishing machine paths or private input manifests. The unchanged Full ablation reproduced the original sixty candidate count, individual-prediction, validation-score and selection records locally; the aggregate reproduction table is released.

Individual record metadata, predictions, message bodies, captures and TLS keys remain local. Full attack refitting, feature/event reconstruction, per-fixture state replay and independent reproduction of exact role diagnostics require those inputs. Released role summaries support their aggregate arithmetic, not reconstruction of individual assignments. The artifact therefore supports code inspection and aggregate arithmetic, not independent full-data replication.

The evidence archive preserves the previous full supplement and its sources. It contains the background literature screening and source locators, coding and adjudication description, reporting examples, deployment maps, and the historical forwarding/concurrency pilot. Its background-extraction report, Sections B and S7, compares naming/association, representation, scheduling, transport cover, execution cover and shared-state isolation. Those measurements keep their original populations and uncertainty; the present main findings use the state control and live timing studies reported here. Artifact contents and limits are indexed in the artifact's top-level \texttt{README.md}.
