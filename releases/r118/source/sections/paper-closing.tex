\section{Ethics and Availability}\label{sec:validity}
\paragraph{Ethics}
Inputs are synthetic, captures are restricted to the experiment, and state-stress writes affect isolated fixtures. The hosted pilot preserved in the evidence archive used account-holder authorization; provider research terms remain unverified, and it supports none of the present main results. Traffic inference can enable profiling; our measurements target synthetic tickets. AI tools assisted extraction, implementation and writing; observations and analyses are preserved. Credentials, hosted transcripts and captures, and third-party full texts are excluded from distribution.

\paragraph{Availability}
The accompanying review artifact provides manuscript sources, versioned collection and analysis implementations, fixture generation, frozen plans and historical timing pools, aggregate tests and costs, the ideal-reference computation, and the source-located background matrix. The added feature-ablation and record-context release includes every candidate's aggregate validation/test count, selected contrasts, event audits and an aggregate arithmetic checker. Its index distinguishes those checks from full-data replication. Per-episode records, individual timestamps and predictions, captures and TLS keys remain local. Independent refitting, feature reconstruction and per-fixture role checks require those withheld inputs. No public release of them is promised.

\section{Conclusion}
Cover generation and execution must be evaluated together. In the measured Agent loop, controller insertion bypasses a model wait and exposes a short-gap signature. Sampled waits remove that shortcut, yet feature ablation shows useful time dependence in three of four history cells. Context removal and joint decoding recover recognition from associated TLS records without supplied test call windows, improving over baselines in five of eight cells. Shadows remain a distinct complete-policy trade-off with more calls and model work. Recording inference, goals, business state and cost on matched executions connects these trace dependencies to the authority and resources a cover policy consumes.
