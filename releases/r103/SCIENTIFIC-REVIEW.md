# R103 scoped scientific integration review

Scope: completed M3 LLM cover results, the current coding-manual pilot and their
integration into the Agent communication side-channel SoK. This is an internal
claim/evidence alignment review, not independent peer review or acceptance advice.
No new experimental collection, fitting, hypothesis family or source search was
introduced in this manuscript revision.

## Main finding

The title and organizing question remain Agent communication side channels.
The positive contribution is a source-grounded map of two execution boundaries,
plus a matched technical measurement of requested-action recognition, goal
completion and full business-state integrity. M3 is first in the main experiment
section and fully documented in Supplement S8. It supplements, rather than
replaces or impersonates, WRA's published research-decoy condition.

The headline compares distinct outcomes on the same 120 held-out fixtures per
cell: live-write Qwen goal/state = 102/7; Llama = 76/3. Their intersections are
95 and 73 goal-complete executions with incorrect global state. Baselines are
114/116 and 82/69, with 0 and 13 completed-but-incorrect executions. The weaker
Llama baseline is visible. Goal predicates require action/object history as well
as the target postcondition; state alone can be correct without that history.

Both state losses survive the original endpoint-specific 12-comparison Holm
families. Goal loss survives only for Qwen. All ten primary recognition
reductions survive their family, but retained position attacks are stronger in
some cells. All 36 contrasts, both learned attacks, frozen and position controls
are shown. Overlapping scores are not called equivalent. Hidden/visible endpoint
comparisons remain inconclusive; path differences also occur between hidden arms.

The scope is deliberately adverse synthetic writes, fixed local 8B planners,
reused phrasings, supplied call windows and destination/byte observations over
MCP TLS1.3/HTTP2. It estimates neither production harm prevalence nor general
Agent privacy. Physical timing and model-link exposure are outside M3. All
formal episodes terminate; a separate development unknown remains excluded and
unretried. The earlier forwarding pilot retains every original nonsignificant
primary comparison and is presented as a separate population.

## Coding correction

C1 is author coding; C2/C3 are dependent non-blind AI passes, with ten C3-exposed
rows. Original agreement and the 34-row sensitivity are arithmetically checked.
No human independence, field validity from kappa=1, or post-adjudication
reliability is claimed. Counts preserve the actual applicable denominators.

Reported outcomes require an assessed endpoint and execution population, and
include source-defined collection success or explicit probe/parser outcomes.
Criterion strength stays separate. Partial records a measured component;
not-located means missing applicable evidence; not-applicable requires no new
execution population. These rules are in Supplement S4.1 and the codebook.

Fifteen outcome disagreements were checked against the versioned source text.
Thirteen statuses change; CG-active remains reported because Section 9.5/Table4
contains live front-running rounds, and CG-transform remains inapplicable for
new outcomes because capture transformation does not re-execute tasks. AI
majority votes do not override these passages. The other twenty-nine rows retain
their prior reading, explicitly disclosed. The resulting 15/42 is descriptive
author extraction, not a settled field-wide reporting rate. Privacy-specific
matched utility and adaptation counts remain 1/4 and 2/4 in different settings;
general execution-outcome recoding does not change that denominator.
