\section{Reproduction scope and evidence archive}
The accompanying artifact separates executable implementations, aggregate arithmetic and retained individual observations. The live-study implementation and frozen training pools identify the five policies. Aggregate attack tables give paired discordance counts and family membership for inspecting McNemar and Holm arithmetic. The record-only scripts retain their original candidate grid and explicitly separate the later exploratory eventizer.

Individual record metadata, predictions, message bodies, captures and TLS keys remain local. Full attack refitting, event reconstruction, per-fixture state replay and exact role diagnostics require those inputs. The artifact therefore supports code inspection and aggregate arithmetic, not independent full-data replication.

The evidence archive preserves the previous full supplement and its sources. It contains the background literature screening and source locators, coding and adjudication description, reporting examples, deployment maps, and the historical forwarding/concurrency pilot. Those measurements keep their original populations and uncertainty; the present main findings use the state control and live timing studies reported here.
