\section{Background Evidence Scope}\label{app:map}
The companion matrix contains forty-four settings from fifteen Agent communication studies. Inclusion requires an LLM to select actions with external feedback and a communication-side inference or evaluated protection. Client, model, tool and coordination interfaces are eligible; transit, recipient and active access remain separate. The bounded window covers first public submissions from 1 January 2024 through 9 November 2026. The evidence archive preserves the exact retrieval, screening, source-version and coding record (R115 Appendix A and Supplement S4--S6).

The settings comprise twenty-two transit observations, ten recipient settings, eleven active settings and one inferred-preference setting. Their source-defined scores are background evidence, not a common benchmark. The four query/workflow privacy settings cited in Section~\ref{sec:gap} come from two sources. The source-located matrix and archived coding record permit inspection of that narrow comparison without treating it as a field-wide frequency.
