R116 separates the measured short-gap shortcut in immediate controller cover from the mixed-context recognition retained by history-based schedules. It reports shadow as a complete policy comparison with different call counts/composition and about twice the model tokens, not an isolated causal advantage of online planning. The record-only primary negative results and strongest tested attacks remain visible. State stress controls are distinct from the live cohort and do not estimate production harms.

This is the local R116 revision of R115 (96b09983fb8c518f635a1db7a951e26698b8b94f), published as an explicit reviewed subset. No new experiments, model calls, attack refits or literature expansion occurred. Draft P0/P1/P2 plans are neither preregistered nor run. The author-side review remains borderline negative; this release does not certify submission readiness or acceptance.

## Distribution boundary

Only papers, LaTeX, source-located background extraction, existing public implementation/training pools, grouped statistics and unrun proposals are included. Per-episode/per-call observations, individual predictions, record timestamps, captures, TLS keys, credentials, model weights and complete local data/review ZIPs remain LOCAL ONLY. Hashes and historical verification receipts identify their own scope; they are not a new scientific certification. Earlier releases and development branches are preserved.
