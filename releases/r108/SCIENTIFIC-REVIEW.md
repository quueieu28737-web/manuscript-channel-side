## Boundaries that affect the conclusion

Receipt timestamps include local scheduling and a 2 ms counting wait. They
are not passive packet arrivals or relay TLS-record timestamps. An endpoint
timing result cannot establish a WAN bypass. The same 120 test fixtures recur
across models and arms; 1,440 executions are not 1,440 independent fixtures.
The analysis was frozen before evaluating the new test timing, while original
M3 test-byte scores were already public. Conditional intervals and paired
test families retain that retrospective scope.

The cover-removal accuracy does not establish equivalence to no cover or
complete loss of every privacy benefit. Nonsignificant semantics comparisons
are not TOST results. A and B2 remain future collection plans. Adverse live
writes remain a constructed state-pressure control, not WRA harms or an
estimate of production prevalence. The forwarding pilot retains its original
nonsignificant primary comparisons and unresolved causal decomposition.
