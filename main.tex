\title{SoK: Transfer Boundaries of LLM and Agent\\Side-Channel Defenses}
\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Side-channel defenses for LLM serving and agent systems are evaluated at one operating point and reused at another. This SoK systematizes the evidence required to carry a defense result across deployment boundaries. Our unit is the evaluated result record: one outcome bound to its target, receiver, intervention, attacker fit, workload and cost object. Across 82 study families and 39 records, eight of 15 same-family pairs concern different primitives, targets or costs; only 17 of 133 related pairs support a source-qualified descriptive comparison. Record-local joins identify one configuration-linked inference--utility--cost package, supplied by two CachePrune records. A six-axis holdout map distinguishes evidence for input, model, topology, hardware, network and temporal changes. Four controlled studies support the synthesis with costed examples of emitter, execution-state, calibration and observation changes. Their results expose distinct re-evaluation needs: timing can recover signal missed by length-only attacks, while 100 training-customer resamples put local GLM return accuracy at 50.0--67.9\%, preventing a model-source interpretation of an unstable calibration contrast. We integrate the literature audit and these scoped measurements into a deployment decision table: identify the changed boundary, retain applicable evidence, and measure the missing binding before reusing a defense claim.
\end{abstract}
