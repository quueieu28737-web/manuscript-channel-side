\title{SoK: Reusing Defense Evidence in LLM and Agent Side Channels}
\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Side-channel defenses for LLM serving and agents intervene in caches, traffic and tool actions. This SoK organizes 19 privacy or attribution mitigation families within a source-linked map of 52 core families. Its central question is which protection, function and cost results describe the same deployment. We identify three changes that alter this decision: a new receiving view, an intervention that acts on different state, and a workload or attacker different from the evaluated one. In the inspected sources, inference outcomes are explicitly bound in 15 of 16 applicable families and task/component outcomes in 3 of 16; accepting partial task/component evidence raises the latter to 5 of 16. Structural preservation can support a narrower function claim when established. A cache-defense example shows how to retain one measured quality/cost package while separating the cost of a different primitive. A decision procedure identifies reusable evidence and required measurements; controlled examples show how attacker adaptation can restore signal after an apparent reduction.
\end{abstract}
