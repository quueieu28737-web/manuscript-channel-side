\author{}

\begin{abstract}
Side-channel defenses for LLM serving and agent systems are evaluated at one operating point and reused at another. This SoK contributes record-level comparison, configuration-level joint binding and deployment decisions for reusing defense evidence. We organize 82 study families and audit 39 result records from 26 families, binding each outcome to its target, receiver, intervention, attacker fit, workload and cost object. First, a complete pair inventory distinguishes source-qualified comparisons, known object mismatches and unresolved bindings. Comparisons within the same paper expose changes of primitive, target and cost that mechanism-level summaries conceal. Second, configuration-linked joins distinguish outcomes that can be interpreted together. CachePrune illustrates this distinction: two recorded outcomes share one inference--utility--cost package at a single configuration. Third, a six-axis holdout matrix connects reported input, model, topology, hardware, network and temporal changes to deployment decisions. The resulting procedure identifies which comparison is supported, which protection, utility and cost outcomes belong together, and which changed boundary requires further measurement. It turns the literature audit into an actionable specification of the evidence needed to reuse a defense claim.
\end{abstract}
