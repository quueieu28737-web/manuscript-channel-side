\title{SoK: Reusing Defense Evidence in LLM and Agent Side Channels}
\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This SoK examines mitigation results reported in empirically attack-evaluated LLM and agent studies. The C1--C5 source frame contains 31 privacy or attribution mitigation records from 19 families; it does not census the full defense literature. We organize evidence by the receiving view, executed intervention and evaluation condition. Functional quality is required when an intervention can change generation, computation or component quality; lossless framing and exact logical preservation instead support a narrower function claim. Under this rule, three of eight applicable families bind a quality measurement, increasing to four when partial evidence is accepted; eleven families are exempt from that quality query. Four agent-associated families provide no linked same-condition end-to-end task score, but their trace, transport and generation interventions require different outcome claims. Configuration-level examples show how to retain measured quality/cost packages and preservation witnesses while separating costs from another primitive. A decision procedure makes these retained claims and remaining deployment measurements explicit; controlled examples also demonstrate attacker-dependent recovery after an apparent reduction.
\end{abstract}
