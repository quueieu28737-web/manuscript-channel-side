\title{SoK: Reusing Defense Evidence in LLM and Agent Side Channels}
\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Side-channel defenses for LLM serving and agents intervene in caches, traffic and tool actions. This SoK maps 82 study families, including 52 core privacy or attribution families; 19 have an evaluated mitigation record under our selection criteria. We systematize those interventions by mechanism and generation layer, and distinguish their measured outcomes from formal guarantees and unresolved residual channels. Across the 19 families, an inference outcome is explicitly bound in 15 of 16 applicable families, utility in 3 of 16, and matched adaptive evaluation in 2 of 10. Accepting partial evidence raises utility to 5 of 16 while matched adaptation remains 2 of 10. Five of eight families with explicit inference and cost place them in different records. Agent privacy and attribution studies provide partial usefulness evidence but no explicit task-utility binding in the four-family subset. Source-linked examples connect these patterns to a deployment choice: retain a measured configuration, separate a cost from a different primitive, or measure state and task outcomes at the chosen execution boundary.
\end{abstract}
