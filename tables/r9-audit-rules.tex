\par\smallskip\raggedright\footnotesize \textbf{B}: all query-required elements explicitly bound to the stated source/configuration; \textbf{P}: some required elements supported but at least one remains unbound; \NRR{}: no relevant report located in the inspected scope; \NRA{}: source unavailable; \NRV{}: binding unverified; --: inapplicable. Examples: an explicit defended fit and test for the same population is B for R4; a defended test with an unspecified fitting condition is P; no fitting or test detail located in the inspected sections is U; an inaccessible source is A, not U. A preservation witness is B only for its stated content invariant, not for task completion. For R4/R5, -- means that a training-fit contrast is inapplicable; it does not establish that non-learning adaptive attacks are irrelevant or absent. For task timeliness or completion, content preservation alone is insufficient. First determine applicability, then assign evidence status. Family \textbf{B} means at least one scoped record satisfies the item, not that every result does.
